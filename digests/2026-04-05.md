### Models & ML Research
**Evolution strategies for local finetuning, RL saturation, and the limits of verifiable rewards**
The technical feed is heavily focused on alternative training regimes, specifically pushing beyond traditional backpropagation and standard SFT to squeeze performance out of consumer hardware and complex environments.

*   **EGGROLL / Forward-pass Finetuning** — Developer @danpacary (amplified by @jdchawla29 and @_vatsadev) demonstrated fine-tuning a 30B parameter model locally on a MacBook Pro by entirely skipping backward passes and gradients. Instead, the process uses "EGGROLL" (an evolution strategy) to randomly perturb weights based purely on forward-pass evaluations, opening a massive new avenue for low-compute local alignment. ([post](https://x.com/jdchawla29/status/2040514663742627921))
*   **The RL Verification Trap** — OpenAI researcher @LiamFedus sparked a deep discussion by noting that while RL applied to verifiable LLM rewards is incredibly powerful, the industry is increasingly falling into a trap: optimizing *only* for tasks where success is easy to automatically check, while abandoning fuzzier, harder-to-verify domains. @amyxlu agreed, comparing the current paradigm to a teacher grading a student on a subject the teacher doesn't understand. ([post](https://x.com/LiamFedus/status/2040462826851201256))
*   **RL vs SFT for UI Agents** — @tzafon_company revealed that standard SFT for computer-use agents hits a hard saturation point after just 100–1,000 examples. However, pivoting to RL yielded a massive 20% absolute jump on the OS-World Chrome benchmark, notably generalizing from environments with zero resemblance to OS-World. ([post](https://x.com/tzafon_company/status/2040472096724443614))
*   **Self-Inference "Gibberish" Gains** — @mgostIH flagged a counter-intuitive finding that training LLMs on their own unfiltered, "gibberish" self-inferenced trajectories actually improves overall model performance across the board, even without formatting or accuracy criteria. ([post](https://x.com/mgostIH/status/2040482920872161351))
