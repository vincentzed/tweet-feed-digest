**LLM-as-a-Verifier hits SOTA, the Mythos takedown expands, and bfloat16 mathematical limits**

### **Infrastructure & Hardware**
**Codex's capacity burn, the acceleration of FP4 serving, and Blackwell quantization yields**
Raw compute allocation remains the ultimate bottleneck, forcing massive software-level optimizations and extreme low-level quantization just to keep API endpoints online.

*   **Attn-QAT unlocks the FP4 era**: Breaking a massive barrier in local deployment, @haozhangml and @haoailab announced `Attn-QAT`, successfully applying Quantization-Aware Training directly to the attention mechanism. This achieves FP4 attention comparable to BF16, fully unlocking end-to-end 4-bit serving on incoming hardware ([post](https://x.com/haozhangml/status/2042441527205773673)).
*   **Codex's exponential capacity burn**: Following the rollout of the new uncapped $100 Pro tier, @thsottiaux from OpenAI admitted the company is scrambling to rack capacity. Codex has rocketed past 3 million weekly users with such violent usage curves that OpenAI mathematically estimates they will be forced to implement another blanket limit-reset in less than two weeks ([post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2042325164902531427)).
*   **Blackwell yields 1.68x gains via combined quantization**: @PyTorch officially validated incoming acceleration curves; a blog by Meta's @vkuzo demonstrated that interleaving NVFP4 and MXFP8 via selective regional compilation in TorchAO pushes up to 1.68x latency improvements natively on Blackwell ([post](https://x.com/PyTorch/status/2041923082353320443)). Demonstrating this in practice, @stochasticchasm and @novasarc01 noted frontier labs are actively using "explicit config knobs" to run disparate layers of massive models in different precisions.
*   **vLLM secures MLPerf validation**: Definitively validating open-source inference infrastructure at scale, @vllm_project and NVIDIA announced the first-ever MLPerf VLM benchmark submitted entirely through the vLLM engine, bypassing proprietary NVIDIA execution harnesses.
*   **Spatial "delta" tokens for World Models**: @gabriberton highlighted an Amazon FAR paper by @tommiekerssies that radically reduces generative environment payload by compressing standard 1024 spatial frame tokens down into a single "delta" token.
*   **Mythos bypasses Blackwell**: @mweinbach clarified that the hyper-scaled Mythos model is not heavily constrained by NVIDIA Blackwell allocations, as Anthropic relies extensively on Amazon Trainium and Google TPUs for massive-state inference pipelines.
