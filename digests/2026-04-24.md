**DeepSeek V4's architecture reset — 1.6T/49B and 292B/13B open-weight, 1M context, and a new post-training recipe that may matter more than the attention stack**

Yesterday's leak is now product, and researchers are reading V4 as a wholesale redirection of the open-weights roadmap rather than an incremental bump. The story breaks in four directions: architecture, post-training, kernels, and compute economics.

- **Official release — @deepseek_ai, @victor207755822, @zizhpan, @JustinLin610, @eliebakouch** — V4-Pro (1.6T total / 49B active) + V4-Flash (292B / 13B), both MIT-licensed, 1M context, live on web/app/API, 484 days after V3 ([post](https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2047516922263285776), [post](https://x.com/victor207755822/status/2047518146689732858)). Trained on 33T tokens with Muon optimizer, novel attention stack combining token-wise compression + DSA2 (NSA + DSA hybrid), MoE via a fused mega-kernel with 6 active of 384 experts, FP4 QAT ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2047519300399837677), [post](https://x.com/JustinLin610/status/2047577502277865631)).
- **Post-training is the real innovation — @finbarrtimbers, @sheriyuo, @novasarc01** — V4 replaces V3.2's mixed RL stage with **online policy distillation**: train specialized experts with SFT+GRPO, then merge them into the final model via on-policy distillation. @sheriyuo: "post-training becomes compositional, not just alignment" ([post](https://x.com/sheriyuo/status/2047519459259359730)). @novasarc01 details the engineering: 10+ teacher models, teacher weights offloaded to centralized storage, naive full-vocab distillation would be prohibitively expensive ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2047576538724622407), [post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2047609010799747430)). @finbarrtimbers initially called it "bland" then retracted ([post](https://x.com/finbarrtimbers/status/2047516717518348456)).
