**Infrastructure & Hardware — the open inference stack catches up, megakernels go mainstream, agentic-KV becomes the bottleneck**

The infrastructure pattern: training kernels are getting bigger and more monolithic, inference engines are getting leaner and more open, and agent traffic is qualitatively different from chat (long, tool-heavy, low output) — both the model layer and serving layer are optimizing for it specifically.

- **TokenSpeed — open-source inference at TRT-LLM speed.** @lightseekorg launched under MIT license claiming "TensorRT-LLM-level performance, vLLM-level usability, built by a lean team in two months" ([post](https://x.com/lightseekorg/status/2052048105412141376)). @vllm_project is exclusive day-0 launch partner via integration of TokenSpeed's MLA library, optimized for long-context multi-turn agentic workloads on Kimi 2.5/2.6 and DeepSeek R1 ([post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2052051210530914510)). @zhyncs42's framing: "<2 months after GTC, we rebuilt the stack end-to-end — kernel → scheduler → modeling → frontend" ([post](https://x.com/zhyncs42/status/2052058547698184413)). vLLM's preliminary benchmarks corroborate the speed claims for low-latency agentic ([post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2052052869847900346)). @tri_dao retweeted, a credibility signal.
- **vLLM × Mooncake — agentic KV at scale.** The detailed problem: agent contexts grow to **80K+ tokens by turn 30, 131:1 input-to-output ratio, single-session KV caches multi-GB**, with 94%+ reusable prefixes that local KV caches evict and cross-instance routing misses. The fix: cluster-wide KV cache pool on Mooncake Store, GPUDirect RDMA to shared DRAM/SSD, SM-free zero-copy, composes with PD disaggregation ([post 1](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2052113331927060840), [post 2](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2052113334426804398)). @james0zan calls Mooncake "the foundational piece everywhere in agentic serving."
