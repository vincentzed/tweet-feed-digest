**Models & ML Research — an autonomous proof, a speed-tier launch, and open-weight architecture experiments**
The week's research arc is about how far general-purpose models now reach into genuinely open problems, set against a crowded model-release calendar.

- **OpenAI** — An internal general-purpose reasoning model produced a ~125-page proof *disproving* Erdős's 1946 planar unit-distance conjecture (the long-assumed `n^(1+o(1))` bound), constructing point configurations with `n^(1+δ)` unit-distance pairs by importing algebraic number theory rather than better geometry. @polynoamial framed it as the headline: "less than 1 year ago frontier models were at IMO gold," and stressed "this is a general-purpose LLM... not a scaffold," and "no it did not use Lean" ([post](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2057178198228586824), [follow-up](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2057179104315670826), [Lean note](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2057216766221115562)). Fields Medalist Tim Gowers (RT'd by @jeremyphoward, "make sure you are sitting down" — [post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2057205310218186823)) and Princeton's Will Sawin verified it, with Sawin pinning δ=0.014 ([OpenAI writeup](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/)). The result drew pushback on provenance: @leothecurious asked why the chain-of-thought needed GPT-rewriting before publication ([post](https://x.com/leothecurious/status/2057348799694017002)), to which @SebastienBubeck replied "there was 0 human involvement. The prompt is in the report." @memecrashes noted a human improved the bound within ~3 hours ([post](https://x.com/memecrashes/status/2057478155246440929)) — which reads less as debunking than as humans-and-AI iterating in real time. @wjmzbmr1 (RT'd by @polynoamial) called it acceptable "without any hesitation" to the Annals ([post](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2057554973987102778)). The most striking framing came from @willdepue: the model is "solving long-standing problems in discrete geometry... still by thinking to itself in plain english? that is easily interpretable?" ([via @charliermarsh](https://x.com/charliermarsh/status/2057220034112496126)).
- **Google DeepMind** — Gemini 3.5 Flash launched at I/O as the new default in the Gemini app and AI Mode. @demishassabis's stat line (RT'd by @JeffDean): better than 3.1 Pro on coding/agentic, "4x faster than other frontier models, 12x faster in Antigravity, 800 tokens/sec, often less than half the cost" ([post](https://x.com/JeffDean/status/2057272410014187693)).
