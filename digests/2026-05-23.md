**Dominant story: the harness is eating the model, validated on the same day by Modal's $355M Series C, an MLSys 2026 kernel-engineering wave, and Microsoft canceling internal Claude Code licenses.** A cluster of independently reported facts converged on one thesis — token-based coding agents are unsustainable at current prices, the "harness" layer (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, Antigravity) is where the value and lock-in now live, and even the labs themselves are conceding that "the model alone is no longer the product" (@gdb).
