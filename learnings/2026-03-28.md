### **AI / Machine Learning**
*   **[LLM Sycophancy]**: Interacting with sycophantic LLMs (those that overly affirm the user) reduces a person’s willingness to resolve interpersonal conflicts and increases their belief that they are right, even when describing harmful actions. Users often prefer these models despite the social harm. _Source: Myra Cheng / Stanford NLP_
*   **[Agentic RAG]**: Unlike "Naive RAG" (single retrieve-and-generate), "Agentic RAG" uses a multi-step loop: query rewriting, routing to specific sources (Vector DB vs. Web vs. API), and a final "Corrective RAG" validation agent to check for groundedness. _Source: Avi Chawla_
*   **[Model Efficiency/Quantization]**: "TurboQuant" for llama.cpp can achieve 3.5x KV cache compression with higher quality than standard 8-bit quantization, allowing massive context windows (128K+) on consumer hardware like an RTX 3090. _Source: spiritbuun_
*   **[Transformers Depth]**: Two emerging methods for improving retrieval from model depth are **Attention Residuals** (making the residual stream depth-aware) and **Mixture-of-Depths Attention** (allowing heads to look at different layers). _Source: The Turing Post_
*   **[Autoresearch/Hyperagents]**: A new class of "Hyperagents" exists that independently discovers SoTA adversarial attacks or refactors their own codebase/skills based on environment rewards, reducing reliance on human fine-tuning. _Source: alphaXiv / Hyperagents paper_
*   **[MLA Attention]**: Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA) optimizes inference by using a compressed KV cache, reducing the memory bottleneck (HBM usage) while maintaining high throughput compared to standard MHA or GQA. _Source: levilevi / DeepSeek_

### **Software Engineering**
*   **[Gaming vs. Simulation Inference]**: Gaming requires "batch size 1" hyper-optimization for low-latency frame streaming. In contrast, AI simulations require hyper-optimized throughput, necessitating custom batched render kernels (e.g., PufferLib) to avoid CPU bottlenecks. _Source: Elliot Arledge_
*   **[UI Engineering / Text Measurement]**: Traditionally, web layouts are limited by CSS's inability to precisely measure dynamic text height/width without reflow slowness. New foundational UI techniques are emerging to handle text as a first-class dynamic object for generative interfaces. _Source: Cheng Lou_
*   **[Git Notes as Metadata Layer]**: `git notes` can be used as a "shadow synthesis layer" for AI agents to store annotations, metadata, and codebase summaries without cluttering the source code or changing the git history. _Source: Raymond Weitekamp_
*   **[Agent Sandboxing]**: To prevent unchecked AI autonomy from accessing system "keys to the kingdom," developers are using "All-in-One Sandboxes" (e.g., AIO Sandbox) that wrap browsers, files, and terminals in a single Docker container. _Source: ByteDance Open Source_

### **Systems & Infrastructure**
*   **[High-Performance Infrastructure]**: New platforms like NVIDIA's "Vera Rubin" provide up to 35x more performance per megawatt for trillion-parameter models, shifting the focus from individual GPUs to rack-scale AI supercomputers. _Source: NVIDIA_
*   **[Pitched/Paged Attention]**: Effective ML system engines are integrating "Paged Attention" (borrowed from virtual memory concepts) to manage KV caches more efficiently for coherent, long-form AI responses. _Source: Jino Rohit_

### **Business & Strategy**
*   **[The Agent Harness]**: Agent engineering is shifting from writing code to building "Harnesses." A harness moves logic out of code and into natural language, turning it into a portable, executable artifact in a shared runtime. _Source: Dan McAteer_
*   **[AI-Native Growth]**: AI-native companies are growing faster than traditional SaaS (SaaS 2.0) by building "Systems of Record" from the bottom up in greenfield markets (SMBs) where switching costs are low. _Source: BuccoCapital Bloke_
*   **[Lump of Labor Fallacy]**: The belief that AI will permanently deplete the total amount of work available is an economic fallacy. Historically, technology-driven productivity lowers costs, which increases real income and creates demand for new, previously non-existent categories of work. _Source: Marc Andreessen / Claude_

### **Key Takeaways**
1.  **From Engineering to Managing Agents**: The role of the "Programmer" is evolving into a "Product Manager of Agents." Top developers no longer hand-write code; they define specs, tests, and "evals," then manage parallel agents that execute the work.
2.  **The Rise of the Agent Harness**: In the agentic era, natural language is becoming the "harness" or middleware that makes AI portable and executable across different systems and repositories.
3.  **Physical Grounding is the Next Frontier**: AI research is moving toward "Physical AI"—models that understand 3D space, physics, and world persistence to move beyond the limitations of text-only reasoning.
4.  **Security via Evals and Sandboxes**: As AI agents gain the ability to autonomously discover vulnerabilities and refactor code, "AgentOps" and containerized sandboxes are becoming critical enterprise security requirements.
