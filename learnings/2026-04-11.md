### Key Takeaways
1.  **Infrastructure Specialization**: We are moving from general cloud computing to specialized "Agentic Infrastructure," characterized by low-latency sandboxes (Modal), multi-modal CLIs (MMX), and hardware-native backends (TorchTPU).
2.  **The Quantization Leap**: The gap between high-precision (BF16) and low-precision (FP4/1-bit) models is closing rapidly due to Quantization-Aware Training, making powerful models more accessible on consumer hardware.
3.  **Benchmark Fragility**: Trust in public AI leaderboards is at an all-time low due to "reward hacking" and intentional data injection, necessitating shifted focus toward human-in-the-loop evaluation and internal "LLM-as-a-verifier" frameworks.
4.  **Strategic Orchestration**: Maximum efficiency is currently found in the "Advisor-Executor" pattern—strategic model pairing that balances high-level reasoning with low-cost execution.
