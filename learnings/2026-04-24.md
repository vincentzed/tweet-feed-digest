# Learnings - 2026-04-24 12:56

## AI / Machine Learning

- **DeepSeek-V4 architecture**: DeepSeek-V4-Pro is a 1.6T total / 49B active MoE trained on 33T tokens with 1M context, using Muon optimizer, DSA (DeepSeek Sparse Attention = NSA + DSA), token-wise compression, head-dim 512, Sparse MQA + SWA, mHC attention, FP4 QAT, and a fused MoE mega-kernel. V4-Lite weighs 285B. Released MIT — the largest open model ever. _Source: @deepseek_ai ([post](https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2047516922263285776)), @eliebakouch ([posts](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2047519300399837677), [post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2047512870359908603)), @yifan_zhang_ ([post](https://x.com/yifan_zhang_/status/2047516808278651036))_
- **Mixed FP4/FP8 checkpoints**: V4 Pro stores MoE expert weights in FP4 and the rest in FP8, so the full 1.6T model fits on a single 8×B200 node — a practical pattern for shipping huge MoEs on commodity nodes. _Source: @LambdaAPI ([post](https://x.com/LambdaAPI/status/2047654086263320965))_
- **20 tokens per parameter heuristic returns**: The classic Chinchilla-era "~20 tokens per parameter" ratio is visible again in V4 Pro (1.6T × 20 ≈ 33T tokens). _Source: @charles_irl ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2047530969008091447))_
- **DeepSeek V4 post-training via on-policy distillation**: V4 replaces V3.2's monolithic mixed-RL stage with specialized expert models (SFT + GRPO) merged into one policy via on-policy distillation — making post-training compositional rather than a single alignment pass. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2047576538724622407)), @sheriyuo ([post](https://x.com/sheriyuo/status/2047519459259359730)), @finbarrtimbers ([post](https://x.com/finbarrtimbers/status/2047516717518348456))_
- **Scaling distillation from many teachers**: V4 distills from 10+ trillion-scale teachers; full-vocab distillation that large is prohibitive, so they offload teacher weights centrally and stream logits, cutting memory/bandwidth per student step. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2047609010799747430))_
- **Batch invariance as a first-class design goal**: The V4 paper emphasizes batch-invariant training (same result regardless of micro-batch layout), suggesting it matters for reproducibility and RL correctness at scale. _Source: @finbarrtimbers ([post](https://x.com/finbarrtimbers/status/2047515065809788940))_
- **Anticipatory routing = stale params**: V4's "anticipatory routing" runs MoE with slightly stale expert parameters to hide routing latency; stabilizes training but may degrade quality. _Source: @suchenzang ([post](https://x.com/suchenzang/status/2047560065843077197))_
- **Attention-sink design persists at frontier**: V4 keeps the attention-sink trick (a single learned scalar that soaks up mass) — a simple mechanism still outperforming more complex data-dependent alternatives. _Source: @gu_xiangming ([post](https://x.com/gu_xiangming/status/2047524906565611527))_
- **Co-training policy and judge**: V4 co-evolves the policy model and the judge/verifier (related to V1-PairRL). Training the verifier alongside the generator is an emerging post-training pattern. _Source: @xiuyu_l ([post](https://x.com/xiuyu_l/status/2047568324989763714))_
- **Muon optimizer validated at scale**: V4 adopted Kimi's Muon optimizer for the majority of modules, confirming Muon's effectiveness at frontier scale across multiple labs. _Source: @crystalsssup ([post](https://x.com/crystalsssup/status/2047550778680963489))_
- **Training stability at scale**: Doubling pretraining tokens from ~15T (V3) to ~33T (V4) introduced instabilities DeepSeek mitigated with mismatched-routing fixes and clamping tricks — scaling tokens is non-trivial. _Source: @suchenzang ([post](https://x.com/suchenzang/status/2047559677316325807))_
- **Compute gap in numbers**: V4-Pro is a ~1e25 FLOP run; V4-Flash ~2.5e24 FLOPs — roughly 40–80x less compute than leading US frontier runs, and <1 day on a 100k GB200 cluster. _Source: @zephyr_z9 ([post](https://x.com/zephyr_z9/status/2047528155976741231)), @_arohan_ ([post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2047531828018380999)), @jakehalloran1 ([post](https://x.com/jakehalloran1/status/2047532930528858152)), @tenderizzation ([post](https://x.com/tenderizzation/status/2047557732287598880))_
- **DeepSeek trained on Huawei Ascend**: V4 appears trained on Ascend, turning a sovereignty constraint into a forcing function for hardware-reshaping ideas — signal that non-NVIDIA training stacks are becoming viable at the frontier. _Source: @Dorialexander ([post](https://x.com/Dorialexander/status/2047632551326413109))_
- **Constraints drive architectural creativity**: DeepSeek, Kimi, and Qwen train strong LLMs under compute/GPU constraints by inventing new attention mechanisms — constraints push efficiency innovation unconstrained labs skip. _Source: @Yuchenj_UW ([post](https://x.com/Yuchenj_UW/status/2047534197993316738))_
- **Neural garbage collection for KV cache**: A language model can be trained end-to-end to decide what to keep vs drop in its own KV cache under a budget — directly optimizing task performance rather than divergence from full attention. _Source: @cwolferesearch ([post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2047476297031631102)), @chrmanning ([post](https://x.com/chrmanning/status/2047102064183202133)), @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2047375282575995146))_
- **Abandoning split-K can still win**: V4's matmul drops split-K in most cases and replaces it with a different optimization set that matches or beats it — "standard" GPU techniques aren't always optimal. _Source: @tenderizzation ([post](https://x.com/tenderizzation/status/2047543297531678951))_
- **Speculative decoding improvement**: Instead of discarding draft tokens on mismatch, you can salvage them — an emerging line of work making speculative decoding meaningfully faster. _Source: @charles_irl ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2047315799094734902))_
- **Hybrid on-device + cloud LLMs**: µLMs (8M–30M params) generate the first 4–8 words on-device in ~55ms, then a cloud LLM continues seamlessly ("commit-and-continue") — masking cloud latency for wearables/constrained devices. _Source: @LukeZettlemoyer ([post](https://x.com/LukeZettlemoyer/status/2046982704713937160))_
- **SonicMoE throughput on Blackwell**: SonicMoE reports 54%/35% higher fwd/bwd TFLOPS than DeepGEMM and 21% higher fwd TFLOPS than the Triton example, while keeping dense-model-level activation memory. _Source: @percyliang ([post](https://x.com/percyliang/status/2047065524245495960))_
- **SAW-INT4 quantization**: Together AI's SAW-INT4 reaches near-BF16 accuracy while preserving INT4's end-to-end speedups. _Source: @tri_dao ([post](https://x.com/tri_dao/status/2047016249973170225))_
- **Super Apriel multi-mixer supernet**: A 15B supernet where every layer carries four token mixers (full attention, sliding-window, Kimi Delta, Gated DeltaNet) — architectural choice moves from train time to request time. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2047341254267093503))_
- **OpenAI Privacy Filter model**: OpenAI's first 2026 open model is a 1.5B bidirectional token-classification adaptation of GPT-OSS, Apache-2.0, that masks PII and keeps 128k context — small enough to run in-browser. Inverting it turns the masker into a PII extractor with structured spans. _Source: @xenovacom ([post](https://x.com/xenovacom/status/2046979474613268993)), @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2046979020890198503)), @chiefofautism ([post](https://x.com/chiefofautism/status/2047582480140562542))_
- **Vision Banana unified model**: Google DeepMind's Vision Banana treats 2D/3D vision tasks (segmentation, edges, depth, surface normals) as image generation, outperforming specialists — a shift toward generative foundations for dense prediction. _Source: @songyoupeng ([post](https://x.com/songyoupeng/status/2047312019976785944)), @sainingxie ([post](https://x.com/sainingxie/status/2047339789926429166))_
- **Decoupled DiLoCo**: DeepMind's Decoupled DiLoCo trains frontier models across multiple data centres while tolerating chip failures — normal synchronous training stalls when a single chip fails. _Source: @GoogleDeepMind ([posts](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2047330981145669790), [post](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2047330984983400793))_
- **MesaNet linear sequence layer**: Google's MesaNet is a Transformer alternative that optimally learns in-context under a fixed memory budget. _Source: @GoogleResearch ([post](https://x.com/GoogleResearch/status/2047630714145776053))_

- **FASTER RL method**: Test-time scaling (best-of-N) in RL is very expensive; FASTER claims to keep the gains while removing the compute cost. _Source: @perryadong ([post](https://x.com/perryadong/status/2047065612258525517))_
- **Self-play plateau in LLMs**: Long-run LLM self-play plateaus much like RL; a new algorithm scales better, letting a 7B match a 100x-bigger model's pass@4. _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2047507993227669545))_
- **Self-improving math AI**: For hard open math, models need to train and self-improve without more external data — conjecturing, proving, and self-guiding with "taste" is a necessary direction. _Source: @tengyuma ([post](https://x.com/tengyuma/status/2047405775677735243))_
- **Scaling laws differ for pretraining vs RL**: Both use power-law language, but the dynamics and saturation behavior are meaningfully different — conflating them is misleading. _Source: @cwolferesearch ([post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2046963975548702786))_
- **Shopify fine-tunes on Python, not a DSL**: Fine-tuning Qwen3-32B on Python (rather than an internal DSL) for tool-calling made their Flow agent 2.2x faster and 68% cheaper — LLMs have far more Python priors than any custom DSL. _Source: @ShopifyEng ([post](https://x.com/ShopifyEng/status/2047339034498752986))_
- **Intelligence per token vs single-number benchmarks**: Intelligence is a function of inference compute — comparing models on a single benchmark is misleading; what matters is intelligence per token or per dollar. _Source: @polynoamial ([post](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2047387675762802998))_
- **Reasoning effort matters**: For GPT-5.5, LOW reasoning (not medium) can be the cost/quality sweet spot for many tasks. _Source: @davis7 ([post](https://x.com/davis7/status/2047416041236836615))_
- **Pretraining still dominates**: Recent model releases (Spud, Mythos) reinforce that pretraining is the cake; RL is the cherry. _Source: @Yuchenj_UW ([post](https://x.com/Yuchenj_UW/status/2047402424605032610))_
- **Pretraining data moats**: Mythos's edge on Humanity's Last Exam appears driven by Anthropic having more pretraining data than OpenAI — capability gaps can trace back to corpus access, not only method. _Source: @itsandrewgao ([post](https://x.com/itsandrewgao/status/2047514480188555475))_

## Key Takeaways

- **DeepSeek V4 is an architectural reset for open weights**: 1.6T/49B MoE, 33T tokens, 1M context, Muon optimizer, DSA + Engram attention, mixed FP4/FP8 checkpoints fitting on a single 8×B200 node, on-policy distillation from 10+ teachers, trained on Huawei Ascend, MIT-licensed with open TileLang kernels. The open-source frontier just redefined its own design space. _Source: @deepseek_ai ([post](https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2047516922263285776)), @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2047512870359908603)), @Dorialexander ([post](https://x.com/Dorialexander/status/2047632551326413109)), @LambdaAPI ([post](https://x.com/LambdaAPI/status/2047654086263320965)), @sheriyuo ([post](https://x.com/sheriyuo/status/2047519459259359730))_
- **Day-0 runtime + RL support is the new launch bar**: vLLM and SGLang + Miles both shipped full inference and end-to-end RL training on V4's launch day — expect "trainer + inference engine on day zero" to be table stakes for major open releases. _Source: @lm_zheng ([post](https://x.com/lm_zheng/status/2047633713530605727)), @BanghuaZ ([post](https://x.com/BanghuaZ/status/2047512679682855053))_
- **Compute gap, not ideas gap**: V4-Pro's ~1e25 FLOPs is <1 day on a 100k GB200 cluster — open-weights labs have the architecture and training know-how; what they lack is the fleet. Moats are shifting from algorithms to sustained compute access. _Source: @jakehalloran1 ([post](https://x.com/jakehalloran1/status/2047532930528858152)), @swyx ([post](https://x.com/swyx/status/2047473701332128030))_
