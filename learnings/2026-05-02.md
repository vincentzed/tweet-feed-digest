## AI / Machine Learning

- **Sparse Autoencoders for Qwen (Qwen-Scope)**: Qwen released Qwen-Scope, an open suite of sparse autoencoders for the Qwen model family that exposes learned features for interpretability and allows steering model outputs by directly manipulating internal features without prompt engineering, plus data classification capabilities. _Source: @Alibaba_Qwen ([post](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2049861145574690992)), @soldni ([post](https://x.com/soldni/status/2049974374258897389))_

- **Inference Compute Scaling Has Not Plateaued**: After 100 million tokens on a multi-step cyber-attack simulation, performance was still increasing. From the report: "Performance on TLO continues to scale with the amount of inference compute spent, and we have not yet observed a plateau with the best models." _Source: @polynoamial ([post](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2049883449327243413))_

- **Embeddings Explained**: Google's Gemini Embedding 2 is their first natively multimodal embedding model, used for video analysis tools and visual shopping assistants. Embeddings convert content (text, images, video) into numerical vectors that capture semantic meaning. _Source: @GoogleAI ([post](https://x.com/GoogleAI/status/2049903687016063456))_

- **Multilingual Embedding Models from IBM**: IBM Granite released two multilingual embedding models (97M and 311M parameters), ModernBERT-based, supporting 200+ languages with 32K context, with day-zero support on Text Embeddings Inference. _Source: @jeremyphoward ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2049697243415106028))_

- **Test-Time Compute for Embeddings = Late Interaction**: A pre-trained embedding model could in principle be improved at inference via multiple encoding rounds, conditional branching, or if-else gates — though this idea already has a name in the literature: "late interaction." _Sources: @hxiao ([post](https://x.com/hxiao/status/2050036307536331081)), @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2050039041580490926))_

- **Efficient Multivector Retrieval at Scale**: Token-aware clustering and hierarchical indexing achieves ~10ms per query for late interaction across hundreds of millions of embeddings — making ColBERT-style retrieval practical at scale. _Source: @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2050055160634708328))_

- **Token-Level PPO-Clip Equivalence**: A "simple masked importance sampling RL loss" is exactly gradient-equivalent to PPO-Clip when computing the mask a particular way — useful for understanding why simpler RL formulations work as well as more complex ones. _Source: @TacoCohen ([post](https://x.com/TacoCohen/status/2049904069641392309))_

- **On-Policy Distillation (OPD) Tradeoffs**: SFT works well early but saturates as the student approaches the teacher; RL can keep compounding through self-generated rollouts. Same-family teachers transfer better in distillation. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2050107463907868896))_

- **Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD)**: Recent LLMs like MiMo-V2-Flash, GLM-5, Nemotron-Cascade 2, DeepSeek-V4, and MiniMax use multi-teacher on-policy distillation, where multiple teachers guide a single student model. _Sources: @cwolferesearch ([posts](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2050347345641116088), [post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2050365896988713025))_

- **OPD Teachers as Process Reward Models**: A useful framing — on-policy distillation teachers function effectively as PRMs (process reward models) because the sampling distribution updates each step as a compounding function of teacher signal. _Source: @willccbb ([post](https://x.com/willccbb/status/2050070615856660494))_

- **RL Generalization Tradeoff**: RL is double-edged — it boosts performance in known territory but causes models to hallucinate by performing tasks they were trained on instead of the actual task in unknown territory. _Source: @fchollet ([post](https://x.com/fchollet/status/2050300365942493547))_

- **RL Generalizes Across Domains**: When OpenAI's RL training produced a "goblin" personality, the fact it transferred broadly indicates RL training generalizes across tasks. _Source: @giffmana ([post](https://x.com/giffmana/status/2049731011773165617))_

- **RL Reward Leakage Creates Personality Artifacts**: OpenAI's "goblin" personality emerged during nerdy personality RL training due to softly conditioned reward signals (likely LLM-as-judge with positive bias) that leaked into the general personality. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2049726261753209271))_

- **Async RL with Multi-Version Streaming (DORA)**: LongCat (Meituan) paper introduces DORA, which keeps multiple policy versions alive at once instead of forcing all rollouts to finish before the next update — long straggler trajectories finish under the policy version they started in, improving throughput in async RL. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2050102682418123213))_

- **RLVR Doesn't Guarantee Causal CoT**: RLVR improves accuracy but doesn't always lead to causal and verifiable chain-of-thought traces, even on reasoning-intensive tasks. Reward shaping plus SFT-before-RL can fix this. _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2049868934422155478))_

- **Binary RAR Reduces Hallucinations**: ICML spotlight paper "Binary RAR" uses RL with binary rewards to address the hallucination–capability trade-off in language models. _Source: @AkariAsai ([post](https://x.com/AkariAsai/status/2049941459999068243))_

- **DR Tulu — RL with Co-Evolving Rubrics**: ICML spotlight paper on long-form deep research using RL with evolving rubrics, achieving SOTA on long-form generation — co-evolving the agent and its reward metric leads to more capable intelligence. _Sources: @AkariAsai ([post](https://x.com/AkariAsai/status/2049941459999068243)), @soldni ([post](https://x.com/soldni/status/2050271946043236521))_

- **Reward Metabolism for Continual Learning**: For continual learning, evals need "reward metabolism" — systems that watch current failures, mutate the verifier, add adversarial cases, and retire stale rubrics to keep pulling the model back toward the real task. _Source: @shyamalanadkat ([post](https://x.com/shyamalanadkat/status/2050289108539646131))_

- **Speculative Decoding for RL Rollouts**: NeMo-RL + vLLM uses speculative decoding to accelerate RL rollouts losslessly: 1.8x throughput at 8B, projected 2.5x end-to-end at 235B — addressing the now-dominant rollout bottleneck in RL post-training. _Source: @vllm_project ([post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2050312463498056085))_

- **P2P Weight Transfer for RL**: SGLang's new P2P weight transfer cuts weight-update times on 1T Kimi-K2 from 53s to 7.2s (7x faster), via source-side CPU replicas + P2P RDMA. NCCL broadcast suffered from redundant transfers, idle trainer ranks, and rigid communication groups that didn't scale. _Source: @ying11231 ([post](https://x.com/ying11231/status/2049698145920299421))_

- **DeepSeek-V4 Inference on Blackwell**: SGLang hits 180 tok/s/GPU on DeepSeek-V4 decode with ~1M context on Blackwell, via Blackwell-specific optimizations exploiting the model's hybrid sparse attention. _Source: @ying11231 ([post](https://x.com/ying11231/status/2050142289906008533))_

- **GB300 + SGLang Throughput vs B200**: Dynamo + SGLang disaggregation on GB300 NVL72 achieves up to 6.5x performance over B200 on DeepSeek-v4 Pro 1.6T. The high-throughput config uses DeepSeek's MegaMoe kernels, which fully fuse and overlap EP dispatch, EP combine, and the GEMMs into a single kernel. _Source: @ying11231 ([post](https://x.com/ying11231/status/2049900044573462966))_

- **SGLang's DeepSeek-V4 Production Stack**: SGLang is a strong production choice for DeepSeek-V4 due to native ShadowRadix prefix caching, HiSparse KV hierarchy, context parallelism, stable compression kernels, and Day-0 MTP/MegaMoE support. _Source: @ying11231 ([post](https://x.com/ying11231/status/2050142271698526304))_

## Science & Research

- **Quantifying Mammogram Discomfort**: UConn researchers used surface electromyography across seven muscle pairs (neck, shoulders, torso) during mammogram screenings, providing a quantitative basis for redesigning the procedure. _Source: @jmirpub ([post](https://x.com/jmirpub/status/2049541742534267020))_
