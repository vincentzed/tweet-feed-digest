# Learnings - 2026-05-03 00:38

## AI / Machine Learning

- **Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD)**: MOPD is the technique used in recent frontier LLMs (MiMo-V2-Flash, GLM-5, Nemotron-Cascade 2, DeepSeek-V4, Mistral). The student learns from multiple teachers via on-policy rollouts rather than fixed labeled data, enabling richer transfer than vanilla SFT. _Sources: @cwolferesearch ([post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2050347345641116088), [post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2050654237076427071)), @yumo_xu ([post](https://x.com/yumo_xu/status/2050363449734209996))_
- **SFT vs RL vs OPD trade-offs**: SFT is great early but saturates once the student nears the teacher; RL keeps compounding through self-generated rollouts. Same-family teachers transfer better in distillation. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2050107463907868896))_
- **RL is a double-edged sword**: In known territory, RL improves performance, but in unknown territory the model tends to hallucinate that it is performing a different task it was trained on — useful framing for why RL'd models go off the rails on novel benchmarks. _Source: @fchollet ([post](https://x.com/fchollet/status/2050300365942493547))_
- **DORA / async RL with multi-version streaming**: Instead of forcing all rollouts to finish before the next update, DORA keeps multiple policy versions alive at once, so long straggler trajectories finish under the policy that launched them — a way to remove the rollout straggler bottleneck in async RL. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2050102682418123213))_
- **Speculative decoding for RL post-training**: NVIDIA Research + NeMo-RL + vLLM show that speculative decoding on rollouts is lossless and gives 1.8× higher throughput at 8B with a projected 2.5× end-to-end speedup at 235B — directly attacks the RL rollout bottleneck (which dominates wall time in RL fine-tuning). _Sources: @vllm_project ([post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2050312463498056085), [post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2050312398293356863))_
- **KL-regularized preconditioners (KL Shampoo / KL SOAP)**: KL-variants outperform their non-KL counterparts because they learn the preconditioners compositionally — a current-edge result in second-order optimization for deep nets. _Source: @_arohan_ ([post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2050593634890993689))_
- **Frontier models still <1% on ARC-AGI-3**: GPT-5.5 scores 0.43%, Opus 4.7 0.18%, GPT-5.4 0.20%, Claude 4.6 0.45%, suggesting current scaling/RL recipes don't transfer to ARC's novelty-heavy tasks. _Source: @fchollet ([post](https://x.com/fchollet/status/2050328852107612559))_
- **Open weights gap is small and shrinking**: The leading open-weights models (Kimi K2, Qwen3.6, etc.) sit just 6 points behind GPT-5.5 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — useful when picking a self-hosted model. _Source: @ArtificialAnlys ([post](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2050096370200281539))_
- **Reverse-engineering frontier model parameter counts is fragile**: A viral paper claimed GPT-5.5 = 9.7T, Opus 4.7 = 4.0T; on review, fixing methodology errors gives GPT-5.5 ≈ 1.5T with a 90% CI of 256B–8.3T — a reminder that "leaked" parameter estimates have huge error bars. _Source: @justanotherlaw ([post](https://x.com/justanotherlaw/status/2050399317782155726))_
- **Late-interaction multivector retrieval at scale**: Token-aware clustering + hierarchical indexing achieves ~10 ms per query over hundreds of millions of embeddings — late interaction (ColBERT-style) is now production-cheap. The community already has a name for "test-time compute on embeddings" (multiple encoding rounds, conditional branching, if-else gates): late interaction. _Sources: @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2050055160634708328), [post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2050039041580490926)), @hxiao ([post](https://x.com/hxiao/status/2050036307536331081))_
- **Tiny transformers can run on FPGA fabric**: Karpathy's MicroGPT was implemented purely in FPGA hardware (no GPU, no PyTorch, no CPU inference loop) and produced 50,000+ tokens/sec — proof that inference doesn't have to live in software. _Source: @luthiraabeykoon ([post](https://x.com/luthiraabeykoon/status/2050620806569361605))_
- **Apple Silicon LLM inference doesn't require MLX**: Moondream's team implemented on-device inference without using MLX, an alternative path for shipping local vision-language models on Macs/iPhones. _Source: @vikhyatk ([post](https://x.com/vikhyatk/status/2050372962952601996))_
- **Old/small base + few examples can crack SWE-bench**: Fine-tuning Alec Radford's vintage LLM on just 250 SWE-bench trajectory examples produced its first SWE-bench solve (a small xarray patch) — small post-training datasets can push surprisingly old base models into agentic territory, and missing programming-language coverage in the base doesn't preclude useful trajectory transfer. _Sources: @rdolmedo_ ([post](https://x.com/rdolmedo_/status/2050665193374732430)), @jyangballin ([post](https://x.com/jyangballin/status/2050690876981326005))_
- **Behavioral probes can beat SAEs for interp; reward-model artifacts are emergent**: The OpenAI "goblin fiasco" was diagnosed by simply talking to models and counting how often they said "Goblin" — a behavioral probe beat SAEs/probing for that case. Quirks like "the reward model likes goblins" are emergent properties of training, not just prompt-side phenomena, and interp work that surfaces them does explanatory work that prompt tweaks cannot. _Sources: @jxmnop ([post](https://x.com/jxmnop/status/2050437965168652344)), @jiaxinwen22 ([post](https://x.com/jiaxinwen22/status/2050641141155164536)), @cwolferesearch ([post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2050236646759575977))_
- **DeepSeek V4 Pro is currently unreliable in agentic settings**: When good it is very good, but high hallucination rates and poor instruction following at the moment make it unusable as a daily driver — likely better after more post-training. _Source: @_xjdr ([post](https://x.com/_xjdr/status/2050403839950631123))_
- **Paper laundering in AI peer review**: Asking an LLM to rewrite your paper can boost AI reviewer scores — current AI reviewers aren't ready to automate peer review, motivating a "science of peer review automation." _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2050303298192765043))_
- **World model evolution**: AI world modeling has shifted from traditional reconstruction methods toward latent-space prediction, with research lines including JEPA, Causal JEPA, and LOWER. _Source: @ylecun ([post](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2050668016271048776))_
- **Code transfer fails zero-shot**: LLMs write good Python/C++ but struggle with uncommon programming languages because of data scarcity. Standard zero-shot cross-language transfer fails for code; Parallel-SFT fixes this by aligning execution-equivalent code across languages. _Source: @nlp_mit ([post](https://x.com/nlp_mit/status/2050426134886584466))_
- **Long-context training is fragile**: Two models with nearly identical short-context performance, trained on the same data, can behave completely differently after long-context extension — short-context evals don't predict long-context behavior. _Source: @sewon__min ([post](https://x.com/sewon__min/status/2050055828867252556))_
- **Planning unlocks small models**: Adding a Blueprint plan upfront took Haiku 4.5 from 18% to 74% solved on a SWE-bench Verified subset where smaller models normally struggle. _Source: @imbue_ai ([post](https://x.com/imbue_ai/status/2050303686879162791))_
- **Co-evolving agent and reward metric (DR Tulu)**: Letting the agent and its reward metric evolve together can lead to more capable intelligence than freezing the reward signal. _Source: @soldni ([post](https://x.com/soldni/status/2050271946043236521))_
- **Multi-agent teams can hurt expert performance**: ICML 2026 finding — putting strong individual models into multi-agent teams can degrade overall task performance ("Multi-Agent Teams Hold Experts Back"). _Source: @james_y_zou ([post](https://x.com/james_y_zou/status/2050275666202087808))_
- **Diffusion language models enable real-time voice**: Mercury 2 (Inception AI) is a non-autoregressive diffusion LLM whose latency profile makes real-time voice agents tractable in a way autoregressive decoding never permitted. _Source: @StefanoErmon ([post](https://x.com/StefanoErmon/status/2050272106475401607))_
- **"Hallucination" needs a sharper definition**: A citation grounded on a real paper but with one wrong author or an off-by-one DOI is a citation with hallucinated *components*, not a hallucinated citation overall — the distinction matters for measurement and mitigation. _Source: @mmitchell_ai ([post](https://x.com/mmitchell_ai/status/2050597817299951706))_
- **LLMs as plausibility engines**: "Every output from an LLM is a hallucination. Sometimes they're right, sometimes they're wrong" — they emit plausible continuations, not verified facts, which is the foundational reliability problem agents are built on top of. _Source: @togethercompute ([post](https://x.com/togethercompute/status/2050271739943211292))_
- **Mistral Medium pricing analysis**: For prefill (compute-bound), a dense 128B should be ~roughly the cost of MoE active parameters; the published input price suggests Mistral Medium's prefill cost is closer to dense-equivalent than to active-only MoE. Useful framework for reasoning about API pricing. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2050609681064763554))_
- **Grok 4.3 sizing inference**: Public benchmarks suggest Grok 4.3 burns compute consistent with a ~2T-parameter model but performs at ~300B level, with likely shape ~500B total / 27B active and ~100T training tokens. _Source: @teortaxesTex ([post](https://x.com/teortaxesTex/status/2050038047710830912))_
- **Verbalized Sampling**: A simple sampling-time change accepted at ICML 2026 with broad downstream impact (already cited in two books and a startup). _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2050307842997981558))_
- **Continual learning research series**: Berkeley/AI launched a continual-learning research seminar series; standing-room kickoff signals renewed institutional focus. _Source: @sarahookr ([post](https://x.com/sarahookr/status/2050343743568720081))_

## Science & Research

- **Neuroscience lacks the open-data flywheel biology has**: Protein structures, plasmids, and cell types in biology have massive shared datasets accelerating research; neuroscience does not — a specific bottleneck a new generation of platforms is trying to fix. _Source: @pelagiamartin ([post](https://x.com/pelagiamartin/status/2050393557912232247))_
- **Radiologist pay and headcount went up, not down, despite AI**: A decade after "AI will replace radiologists," US radiologists earn >$500K/year and employment continues to grow — reading scans is a task, not a job, and cheaper tasks expanded demand for the job. _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2050302579599376579))_
- **MBE → TLE is a real generational shift**: Molecular beam epitaxy (since 1968) is the gold standard for atomically-precise thin-film growth — it's how every III-V laser diode, quantum-Hall device, and phone-RF HEMT is built. Thermal laser epitaxy (TLE) is positioned as its successor: instead of heating elemental sources in an MBE chamber, it uses lasers on the source materials, with implications for throughput and material range. _Sources: @JosephJacks_ ([post](https://x.com/JosephJacks_/status/2050437353827963285), [post](https://x.com/JosephJacks_/status/2050351744044175363))_
- **Quantifying mammogram discomfort**: UConn researchers used surface electromyography across 7 muscle pairs (neck, shoulders, torso) during screening to objectively quantify strain — a measurement-first path to redesigning the procedure. _Source: @jmirpub ([post](https://x.com/jmirpub/status/2049541742534267020))_
- **Anesthetic argument for quantum consciousness**: Stuart Hameroff's case rests on three observations about anesthetics: (1) selectivity — they block consciousness while sparing nonconscious brain activity; (2) they bind via quantum effects; (3) full reversibility once gases dissipate. The microtubule "fractal time crystal" theory builds on these. (Speculative; included for the empirical observations.) _Sources: @StuartHameroff ([post](https://x.com/StuartHameroff/status/2050446316975833378)), @JosephJacks_ ([post](https://x.com/JosephJacks_/status/2050255345583558974))_
- **AI co-scientist for microbiome**: Stanford HAI funded an AI co-scientist agent for microbiome research in collaboration with the Sonnenburg Lab — concrete example of agentic systems in lab science. _Source: @james_y_zou ([post](https://x.com/james_y_zou/status/2050247964015436195))_
- **Paperclip adds arXiv access**: The Paperclip MCP/CLI tool now indexes arXiv alongside bioRxiv, medRxiv, and PubMed Central — useful for agents needing recent literature access. _Source: @james_y_zou ([post](https://x.com/james_y_zou/status/2050010125663129654))_

## Key Takeaways

- **Planning is a free upgrade for small models**: Adding a Blueprint planning step took Haiku 4.5 from 18% to 74% on a SWE-bench Verified subset — upfront structure can substitute for raw model size. _Source: @imbue_ai ([post](https://x.com/imbue_ai/status/2050303686879162791))_
- **RL post-training has rollout-bottleneck fixes ready to deploy**: Speculative decoding (1.8×–2.5× throughput on NeMo-RL + vLLM) and async multi-version streaming (DORA) are immediately useful if you're scaling RL training. _Sources: @vllm_project ([post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2050312463498056085)), @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2050102682418123213))_
- **Long-context behavior is not predicted by short-context evals**: Two models with identical short-context performance and identical training data can diverge wildly after long-context extension — long-context capability has to be evaluated directly, not extrapolated. _Source: @sewon__min ([post](https://x.com/sewon__min/status/2050055828867252556))_
- **SGLang on Blackwell is a real reference architecture**: 180 tok/s/GPU on DeepSeek-V4 decode at ~1M context, with documented technique stack (ShadowRadix, HiSparse KV, context parallelism, MTP/MegaMoE) — concrete blueprint for production-scale frontier-model serving. _Source: @ying11231 ([post](https://x.com/ying11231/status/2050142289906008533))_
- **Open-source LLMs are 6 index points behind GPT-5.5 — and dozens of times cheaper**: The capability gap is now small enough that for most user tasks, capable + cheap + stable + controllable beats marginal frontier — self-hosting is a real option. _Sources: @ArtificialAnlys ([post](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2050096370200281539)), @suchenzang ([post](https://x.com/suchenzang/status/2050506151284854833))_
