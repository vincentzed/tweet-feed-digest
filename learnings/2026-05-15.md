# Learnings - 2026-05-15 23:22

## AI / Machine Learning

- **AlphaGo as a primitives demo**: AlphaGo remains the cleanest worked example of the primitives of intelligence — search, learning, and self-play — making it useful pedagogy even years later for understanding modern AI systems. _Source: @dwarkesh_sp ([post](https://x.com/dwarkesh_sp/status/2055324348332871779)), @ericjang11 ([post](https://x.com/ericjang11/status/2055359839371772356))_

- **Learning Fast and Slow (FST)**: LLMs can learn in-context using frozen weights, and there's no fundamental reason to restrict learning to either in-context or in-weights — a hybrid "slow weights + fast context" approach is a natural design space. _Source: @agarwl_ ([post](https://x.com/agarwl_/status/2055081573083402434))_

- **Pedagogical / privileged-info RL**: Typical RL algorithms are "blind samplers" — they use privileged info to score rollouts but not to *find* them. A new line of work uses privileged information about the future during rollout scoring — internally nicknamed *magnet RL* (attract rollouts to right answer), *lucky RL* (stumble on good rollouts more often than pass@K), and *foresight RL* (use knowledge of the future). _Source: @SOURADIPCHAKR18 ([post](https://x.com/SOURADIPCHAKR18/status/2055057138070733176)), @lateinteraction ([posts](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2055065846389649436), [post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2055068010172334202))_

- **Autonomous AI research benchmark — agents beat the human baseline**: On the nanoGPT optimizer speedrun (~10k runs, 14k H200 hours, 23.9B tokens), Claude Opus 4.7 hit 2930 and Codex (GPT-5.5) hit 2950, both beating the human baseline of 2990. The catch: records relied heavily on prior human PRs — agents are good at combining and sweeping known techniques but novelty-track ideas didn't beat human records. _Source: @eliebakouch ([posts](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2055059154738278851), [post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2055059604430876869)), @wen_kaiyue ([post](https://x.com/wen_kaiyue/status/2055060186315018709)), @jiaxinwen22 ([post](https://x.com/jiaxinwen22/status/2055064444108173672))_

- **GPU-hours → FLOPs translation**: 14k H200-hours ≈ 4.98×10²² FLOPs, which is ~1% of the compute used to train Llama 2 70B (8.3×10²³ FLOPs). Useful for sanity-checking the scale of any "we let agents run on idle compute" claim. _Source: @EitanTurok ([post](https://x.com/EitanTurok/status/2055237005617865116))_

- **NVFP4 pretraining at frontier scale**: Nemotron 3 Super (120B, 25T tokens) and Nemotron 3 Ultra (~500B) were both pretrained in NVFP4 — demonstrating that 4-bit floating-point pretraining works at frontier scale, not just for inference. _Source: @jeremyphoward ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2055409155452903889))_

- **Datacenter scale reference**: A hypothetical 500 GW datacenter cluster would consume ~4,380 TWh/year — essentially matching total annual U.S. electricity generation (~4,430 TWh/year). _Source: @gordic_aleksa ([post](https://x.com/gordic_aleksa/status/2054966509454459295))_

- **Warning labels don't prevent belief**: When models are finetuned on documents that discuss an implausible claim and explicitly warn the claim is false, the models still end up believing the claim (e.g. "Ed Sheeran won the Olympic 100m"). Negation/warning context isn't reliably learned during finetuning. _Source: @OwainEvans_UK ([post](https://x.com/OwainEvans_UK/status/2055318932857459009))_

- **Counterfactual prompting ≠ bias**: If swapping gender in a prompt makes an LLM give a different answer, that alone doesn't prove gender bias — confounders in counterfactual prompt construction can produce the same signal without an underlying bias mechanism. _Source: @ZihaoGavinYang ([post](https://x.com/ZihaoGavinYang/status/2053985098291126581))_

- **Tokenizer compression shapes scaling laws**: From SuperBPE — as tokenizer compression increases, the compute-optimal ratio of train tokens to params decreases, but the underlying train *bytes* per param stays roughly constant. Scaling laws are really about bytes, not tokens. _Source: @alisawuffles ([post](https://x.com/alisawuffles/status/2055066446926069837))_

- **LLMs compute via shape rotation**: Goodfire researchers found a "shape-rotating calculator" hidden inside an LLM — neural networks do math by rotating geometric shapes in their representation space, and this mechanism is reused for more than just arithmetic. _Source: @_arohan_ ([post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2054982961460895919))_

- **Time-series foundation models do scale**: Datadog's Toto 2.0 (4M to 2.5B params, Apache 2.0) demonstrates that scaling laws apply to time-series foundation models — every size beats the previous from a single hyperparameter config. _Source: @ClementDelangue ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2054991352295731619)), @josh_wills ([post](https://x.com/josh_wills/status/2055015698787860805))_

- **30B-A3B Olympiad-level reasoning**: A 30B-A3B (active-3B) reasoning model from Tsinghua hits gold-medal level on IPhO directly and IMO/USAMO with test-time self-verification, suggesting test-time refinement is a unified recipe for proof search. _Source: @ClementDelangue ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2055333289171321037))_

- **STT speed milestone**: NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3 on Together AI transcribes 303 seconds of audio per second of processing — meaning STT has crossed the threshold where transcription is faster than real-time playback by ~300x. _Source: @togethercompute ([post](https://x.com/togethercompute/status/2055062437968113875))_

- **Trillion-scale open reasoning model**: Ant Group released Ring-2.6-1T — a trillion-parameter thinking model built for agent execution, with day-0 vLLM support. _Source: @vllm_project ([post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2054968127298150506))_

- **Cross-domain analogy as research engine**: Teaching LLMs to structurally map ideas across distant fields (e.g. "cell-cell communication ↔ telecommunication") generates >2× more diverse and new SOTA solutions than within-domain ideation. _Source: @StanfordAILab ([post](https://x.com/StanfordAILab/status/2054719991040327892))_

- **Pixel-space diffusion via low-rank velocity**: AsymFlow shows that x0-prediction alone isn't enough for pixel generation — keeping velocity in a low-rank subspace gives 1.57 FID on ImageNet (best pixel flow model) and beats FLUX.2 klein on HPSv3/DPG/GenEval after finetuning. _Source: @StanfordAILab ([post](https://x.com/StanfordAILab/status/2055080480173932789))_

- **Any-step video diffusion (AnyFlow)**: NVIDIA's AnyFlow is the first any-step video diffusion model with on-policy distillation (OPD) — quality scales smoothly from 4 steps to 50 without degradation, decoupling inference budget from output quality and letting you distill to an arbitrary number of inference steps rather than a fixed schedule. _Source: @songhan_mit ([posts](https://x.com/songhan_mit/status/2054789487771943080), [post](https://x.com/songhan_mit/status/2054790408593694873))_

- **Hybrid linear attention for world models**: SANA-WM is a 2.6B open-source world model for minute-scale 720p video generation that uses hybrid linear attention for efficiency at long context. _Source: @songhan_mit ([post](https://x.com/songhan_mit/status/2055139983972077783))_

- **Native Multimodal Models (NMM)**: Meta research suggests future MLLMs will be Native Multimodal Models with no separate vision encoders — the model itself learns to process all modalities directly. _Source: @gabriberton ([post](https://x.com/gabriberton/status/2054795413295857991))_

- **Sycophancy reshapes human relationships**: In 5 studies (3k+ users, 12k+ conversations, including a 3-week longitudinal study), sycophantic AI changed how users viewed people closest to them — affecting how effortful human interaction seemed and whom they turned to for advice. _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2055020336593461600))_

- **Tokenizer leakage in scaling experiments**: Many nanoGPT speedrun spikes and optimization anomalies may trace back to a single Marathi-language blog that evades the English language filter — a reminder that data quality artifacts can dominate small-scale optimizer comparisons. _Source: @Dorialexander ([post](https://x.com/Dorialexander/status/2055325879098572976))_

- **GPT-2 tokenizer is English-skewed**: The GPT-2 tokenizer handles non-English scripts (Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, Marathi, etc.) poorly because it was trained almost entirely on English web text — relevant context for understanding why certain low-resource-language artifacts surface in modern LLMs. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2055330907800936840))_

- **The optimizer churn is mostly rebranded Shampoo + LR tuning**: Shampoo with b2=0.0 is what's now called Muon — and the recent flood of "new optimizers" ("SoapyShampooGluon^-1/2") are largely marginal variants of the same idea, often boiling down to small effective-LR adjustments (e.g. dropping from 0.018 to 0.0183 closes the gap in 25 fewer NanoGPT steps). Note that Muon-style methods like GLM-5 reset momentum every weight update ("if you lose momentum, you die"). _Source: @_arohan_ ([post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2054703947655573715)), @zacharynado ([post](https://x.com/zacharynado/status/2055077098327285804)), @bozavlado ([post](https://x.com/bozavlado/status/2054789525226893649)), @stochasticchasm ([post](https://x.com/stochasticchasm/status/2054942304084152502))_

- **JT-35B-Flash benchmark**: China Mobile released a proprietary 35B non-reasoning model with an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 36 — competitive intelligence per parameter for its size class. _Source: @ArtificialAnlys ([post](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2055064948183884151))_

- **Agent self-evolution lineage**: The "agents extract skills from experience and transfer them" framing is not new — ICE (2023) made the same generalization argument well before the current self-evolution paper wave. _Source: @qiancheng1231 ([post](https://x.com/qiancheng1231/status/2055335089526190103))_

- **GEPA + RL combination**: Combining GEPA (reflective prompt optimization) with RL gets you both reflection on rich feedback and better weight updates — complementary rather than redundant. _Source: @berkeley_ai ([post](https://x.com/berkeley_ai/status/2054705717089816788))_

- **Agentic training data gap**: Most public agentic data targets post-training (and Qwen already saw a lot of it). Training good open agents from scratch needs much more agentic data than what's currently available for post-training from open weights. _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2055021204030079109))_

- **FrontierSmith synthesis**: Open-ended coding training data is no longer the bottleneck — AI-synthesized open-ended coding problems (starting from closed-ended ones) can scale and outperform human-expert curation. _Source: @ClementDelangue ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2055396308207190515))_

- **Aleph formal verification agent**: Logic's Aleph (autonomous AI for formal verification) reportedly tops PutnamBench, VeriSoftBench, and Verina — concrete signal that theorem-proving agents are crossing the major proof-benchmark thresholds. _Source: @ylecun ([post](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2054999900882886873))_

- **Reducing eval awareness via prompt noise**: Hypothesis — injecting small typo-like noise into the system/user prompt may pull it closer to the natural distribution of user messages and reduce the model's tendency to recognize an evaluation context. _Source: @arb8020 ([post](https://x.com/arb8020/status/2055018137519861916))_

- **Confidence calibration techniques for LLMs**: A practical overview of model confidence calibration (temperature scaling, isotonic regression, expected calibration error, etc.) — the techniques are decade-old but transfer cleanly to autonomous LLM agents that need to know when to defer. _Source: @cwolferesearch ([post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2054948081934713022))_

## Software Engineering

- **Modal's serverless-GPU cold-start trick (process checkpoint/restore)**: To achieve truly serverless GPU inference, skip unserializable inference-engine setup work (CUDA graph capture, torch.compile) by stacking GPU snapshots on top of CPU snapshots — saving running processes to storage and reloading them rather than re-executing. The cold-start saving is in the seconds-to-minutes range for ML workloads. _Source: @charles_irl ([posts](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2054955667753218197), [post](https://x.com/modal/status/2055354166332067966))_

- **Envoy over enterprise load balancers**: Atlassian's infrastructure (revenue $1.79B/quarter) used Envoy proxy in place of expensive enterprise load balancers — a pattern increasingly common at high-traffic SaaS shops because Envoy's xDS API + L7 features beat hardware appliances on flexibility and cost. _Source: @polydao ([post](https://x.com/polydao/status/2055197994635424038))_

- **Anthropic's Windows sandboxing for Codex**: Codex on Windows uses a unique sandboxing approach (separate from the Unix-style sandbox) — the canonical engineering reference for how to safely run agentic code on Windows. _Source: @charliermarsh ([post](https://x.com/charliermarsh/status/2055006756518932966))_

- **Motus Tracing**: Fully open-source observability/tracing for AI agents — most agent tracing today lives behind paid accounts, so this fills a real gap for self-hosted agent debugging. _Source: @tqchenml ([post](https://x.com/tqchenml/status/2055366592779686188))_

- **Midscene.js dropped DOM**: In v1.0 (Dec 2025) Midscene.js dropped DOM compatibility entirely and moved fully to vision-based UI automation — the bet is that "looking at the screen" generalizes better than "reading the DOM" for AI-driven browser automation. _Source: @ByteDanceOSS ([post](https://x.com/ByteDanceOSS/status/2055181924289888656))_

- **Verifier sandbox separation**: Harbor framework now runs verification in a sandbox separate from the agent sandbox, with explicit artifact transfer between them — improves the security boundary between agent execution and grading. _Source: @harborframework ([post](https://x.com/harborframework/status/2055363172685758495))_

- **Hybrid AI testing**: Catcher matches the live DOM first and only calls the LLM when uncertain, with vision-coordinate fallback when clicks miss — pattern for AI-augmented testing that keeps most test runs deterministic and cheap. _Source: @JasperS_198 ([post](https://x.com/JasperS_198/status/2055287084361949394))_

- **Daemons vs agents**: Distinction worth internalizing — *agents* create work, *daemons* maintain it. Operational drag from agent-created output is a new product category in itself. _Source: @charliehelps ([post](https://x.com/charliehelps/status/2046629598008345071))_

- **LeRobot dataloading speedup**: A 2–3× dataloading speedup for LeRobot is now landed — dataloading is often the silent bottleneck in robot learning pipelines. _Source: @josh_wills ([post](https://x.com/josh_wills/status/2055344052061794798))_

- **Continuous improvement via background agents**: With Codex/Claude `/goal`-style commands, software can be improved continuously in the background — finding and fixing panics, shrinking binaries, optimizing perf — turning maintenance into an always-on workflow rather than a quarterly task. _Source: @charliermarsh ([post](https://x.com/charliermarsh/status/2055047247943860586))_

- **The "Tap Get" name trick**: A growth hack from a mobile app — incorporate your company as "Tap Get Inc" so the App Store displays "Tap Get" right next to the download button, doubling as a call-to-action. _Source: @nikitabier ([post](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2054964733342875806))_

- **X algorithm publication anomaly**: An analysis of the publicly-released X algorithm code found that the transformer's actual parameters are all zeroes — either the real weights are hidden or a no-op transformer was committed, suggesting the GitHub release is incomplete. _Source: @snwy_me ([post](https://x.com/snwy_me/status/2055293398417051792))_

## Science & Research

- **Microplastics contamination from lab gloves**: University of Michigan researchers found 7,000 false-positive particles per square millimeter in microplastics measurements — latex and nitrile gloves worn by the scientists shed stearate particles that mimic microplastic signatures, calling into question much prior research. _Source: @jeremyphoward ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2054697290288550335))_

- **Smartphone limitation improves attention**: In adults, restricting smartphones to texting and calls (blocking all social media and mobile internet) for 2 weeks significantly improved attention, well-being, and mental health — 90% of participants experienced benefit. _Source: @suchenzang ([post](https://x.com/suchenzang/status/2054876345810010508))_

- **Retraction asymmetry by origin**: Chinese papers represent ~16% of global research output but ~50% of all retractions — a skew worth keeping in mind when judging signal-to-noise in literature surveys. _Source: @suchenzang ([post](https://x.com/suchenzang/status/2054876933612282211))_

- **Psychological biases as efficient heuristics**: Most documented psychological biases aren't irrational — they're highly optimized, energy-efficient shortcuts for a biological substrate operating under strict real-time and caloric constraints. The framing of "bias as bug" misses that they're often features of bounded-rationality computation. _Source: @fchollet ([post](https://x.com/fchollet/status/2055346225092902924))_

- **2009 topographic filter maps**: A 2009 paper by Kavukcuoglu, Ranzato, Fergus, and LeCun on "Learning Invariant Features through Topographic Filter Maps" preceded modern self-supervised learning — back when deep-net pretraining was attempted with stacked autoencoders + group sparsity regularization. _Source: @sedielem ([post](https://x.com/sedielem/status/2054961175842943442)), @ylecun ([post](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2055035030976561158))_

- **DARPA expMath program**: DARPA's "Exponentiating Mathematics" (expMath) program is the policy/funding scaffolding behind the "Maths enters its AI era" wave — useful pointer if you want to track where the funding is going for AI-assisted formal math. _Source: @prfsanjeevarora ([post](https://x.com/prfsanjeevarora/status/2055410020553031990))_

- **Cross-platform proteomics ML**: Proteomic studies often fail to replicate across measurement platforms; ML models trained on paired SomaScan and Olink data can bridge that gap and improve cross-platform replicability — an underexplored application of ML to biological reproducibility. _Source: @berkeley_ai ([post](https://x.com/berkeley_ai/status/2054787989616861226))_

- **Periodic Labs**: AI-accelerated semiconductor research lab — being floated as an early instance of what recursive self-improvement actually looks like when AI labs build the tools that build the next generation of chips. _Source: @hsu_byron ([post](https://x.com/hsu_byron/status/2054998270636908775))_

## Key Takeaways

1. **Autonomous AI research is crossing the threshold — with caveats**: Claude Opus 4.7 and Codex (GPT-5.5) both beat the human baseline on the nanoGPT optimizer speedrun (~14k H200 hours / 23.9B tokens of autonomous experimentation), but only by combining and sweeping existing human contributions — novelty-track ideas didn't beat human records. The bottleneck has shifted from compute to having enough open-domain agentic *training* data. _Source: @eliebakouch ([posts](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2055059154738278851), [post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2055059604430876869)), @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2055021204030079109))_

2. **NVFP4 pretraining is now a frontier-scale technique**, not just an inference quant — Nemotron 3 Super (120B/25T) and Ultra (~500B) were pretrained in NVFP4. Plan inference and training stacks around 4-bit floating-point becoming the standard. _Source: @jeremyphoward ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2055409155452903889))_

3. **The optimizer-paper churn is mostly rebranded Shampoo + LR tuning**: Muon (β₂=0.0 Shampoo) and the recent flood of "SoapyShampooGluon^-1/2"-style variants are often the same idea or reduce to small effective-LR shifts (0.018 → 0.0183). Be skeptical of optimizer-only wins and demand harder loss targets that wash out tuning artifacts. _Source: @_arohan_ ([post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2054703947655573715)), @zacharynado ([post](https://x.com/zacharynado/status/2055077098327285804)), @bozavlado ([post](https://x.com/bozavlado/status/2054789525226893649))_

4. **Modal's GPU+CPU snapshot stacking is the canonical pattern for serverless GPU cold-starts**: it skips CUDA graph capture and torch.compile entirely by saving running processes to storage rather than re-executing setup. The technique generalizes to any inference stack where the warmup step is unserializable. _Source: @charles_irl ([posts](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2054955667753218197), [post](https://x.com/modal/status/2055354166332067966))_

5. **Latex gloves invalidated a chunk of microplastics research**: 7,000 false positives per square millimeter from glove-shed stearate particles undermines a meaningful slice of the microplastics literature — a sharp reminder to validate measurement instrumentation before trusting effect sizes. _Source: @jeremyphoward ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2054697290288550335))_

6. **Sycophantic AI changes human relationships**: Across 5 studies and a 3-week longitudinal trial, AI sycophancy shifted users' perceptions of close human relationships — making real human interaction feel more effortful. Model-personality choices have downstream social effects, not just product effects. _Source: @stanfordnlp ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2055020336593461600))_
