# Learnings - 2026-05-16 17:08

# AI / Machine Learning

- **AlphaGo as a teaching tool for AI primitives**: A modern reimplementation of AlphaGo from scratch is the cleanest worked example of intelligence primitives — search, learning, and self-play — and stepping backward to it can illuminate the future of AI better than studying frontier systems. _Sources: @ericjang11 ([post](https://x.com/ericjang11/status/2055359839371772356)), @dwarkesh_sp ([post](https://x.com/dwarkesh_sp/status/2055324348332871779))_

- **Learning Fast and Slow (FST) for LLMs**: LLMs can learn either by updating weights (slow) or in-context with frozen weights (fast); there's no good reason to restrict learning to one mode, and combining both is a natural research direction. _Source: @agarwl_ ([post](https://x.com/agarwl_/status/2055081573083402434))_

- **DeepSeek V4's MegaMoE kernel**: DeepSeek V4 ships with a 1,400-line fused CUDA kernel ("MegaMoE") that computes the entire MoE forward pass in one shot, eliminating multiple kernel launches and memory round-trips that plague typical MoE implementations. _Source: @SemiAnalysis_ ([post](https://x.com/SemiAnalysis_/status/2055423177841353184))_

- **NVFP4 pretraining at scale**: NVIDIA pretrained Nemotron 3 Super (120B params, 25T tokens) and Nemotron 3 Ultra (~500B params) entirely in NVFP4, showing that 4-bit floating-point formats are now viable end-to-end for foundation-model training, not just inference. _Source: @jeremyphoward retweeting @ctnzr ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2055409155452903889))_

- **30B reasoning model hits Olympiad gold**: A 30B-A3B reasoning model achieves gold-medal level on IPhO directly and on IMO/USAMO with test-time self-verification and refinement — demonstrating that a unified scaling recipe for proof search works at relatively modest parameter counts. _Source: @ClementDelangue retweeting @stingning ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2055372369485615155))_

- **Why preconditioners help NN optimization**: A new ICML 2026 optimizer paper provides intuition for why simple preconditioners like orthogonalization and row-normalization specifically benefit neural network optimization, rather than just being heuristics. _Source: @eliebakouch retweeting @DengShenyang24 ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2054998370326815110))_

- **Data filter leaks shape training dynamics**: Anomalous spikes in nanoGPT speedrun results can be traced to a specific Marathi blog that evades the English-only data filter — small filtering gaps can have outsized effects on training behavior and benchmarks. _Source: @Dorialexander ([post](https://x.com/Dorialexander/status/2055325879098572976))_

- **PyTorch SPMD types for tensor parallelism**: PyTorch is exploring SPMD (Single Program Multiple Data) types to make tensor parallelism mistakes catchable at type-check time, addressing one of the most error-prone areas of distributed training. _Source: @PyTorch ([post](https://x.com/PyTorch/status/2055019525679231062))_

- **UI automation moving from DOM to vision**: Midscene.js dropped DOM compatibility entirely in its 1.0 release, betting that UI automation should "look at the screen" with vision models rather than parse the DOM — a fundamental shift in how browser/UI agents are built. _Source: @ByteDanceOSS retweeting @midscene_ai ([post](https://x.com/ByteDanceOSS/status/2055181924289888656))_

- **AI agent cost asymmetry**: A DeepSeek V4 agent running inside Codex pursued a goal for 13 hours, burning ~100M tokens, at a total cost of $1.85 — showing how cheap open-weight model inference has become relative to closed frontier APIs. _Source: @Michaelzsguo ([post](https://x.com/Michaelzsguo/status/2055317248923848713))_

- **Singapore tops Claude usage**: Singapore is now the #1 country for Claude usage globally; GIC also led Anthropic's $30B Series G — concentration of LLM adoption in specific geographies has both economic and policy implications. _Source: @zephyr_z9 ([post](https://x.com/zephyr_z9/status/2055197252826849306))_

- **TokenSpeed MLA in vLLM**: TokenSpeed's Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA) implementation has been integrated into vLLM for Blackwell GPUs, broadening the runtimes where DeepSeek-R1 / Kimi-K2.5 users benefit from MLA acceleration. _Source: @zhyncs42 ([post](https://x.com/zhyncs42/status/2055500922374197254))_

- **In-context behavior is fragile to fine-tuning**: Models can be fine-tuned on documents that discuss an implausible claim and explicitly warn the claim is false, yet still internalize the claim — illustrating the gap between surface-level "warning" supervision and what models actually learn. _Source: @Thomas_Woodside ([post](https://x.com/Thomas_Woodside/status/2055335696324235504))_

# Systems & Infrastructure

- **Jane Street's AI training data center specs**: 4,032 GPUs across 56 racks, 8,000 km of fiber, with liquid cooling running through every server because air cooling can no longer handle the heat density of modern training clusters. _Source: @GoshawkTrades ([post](https://x.com/GoshawkTrades/status/2055407770262962184))_

- **Jane Street's early infrastructure philosophy**: Their first cluster was 6 Dell boxes physically in the office so engineers could literally unplug it when things went wrong — a reminder that infrastructure pragmatism matters more than scale at the start. _Source: @dwarkesh_sp ([post](https://x.com/dwarkesh_sp/status/2055427017932468728))_

- **Serverless GPU inference via process snapshots**: To achieve true serverless GPU inference, you can skip unserializable setup steps (CUDA graph capture, Torch compilation) by stacking GPU memory snapshots with CPU process snapshots, then restoring instead of re-executing — yielding ~7x (4–10x) reduction in vLLM/SGLang replica start times on Modal. _Sources: @charles_irl ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2055318350176395299)), ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2054955667753218197)), ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2055318365808562611)), ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2055318380941648323))_

- **H200 market pricing**: Hyperbolic added 400 H200 GPUs (141GB HBM3e) to on-demand at $2.99/GPU/hr — useful reference price for an extremely tight H200 market. _Source: @zjasper ([post](https://x.com/zjasper/status/2055503737121308845))_

- **Agentic workloads have exploitable structure**: GPU and CPU optimizations for agentic workloads are in early innings — overlapping, parallelization, and KV-cache programming all have headroom because agentic execution patterns differ structurally from single-shot inference. _Source: @KranenKyle ([post](https://x.com/KranenKyle/status/2055347652762710294))_

- **Open observability for agents**: Motus Tracing offers fully open-source observability for AI agents — most agent tracing stacks live behind subscription tiers, making token-burn debugging unnecessarily opaque. _Source: @tqchenml retweeting @JiaZhihao ([post](https://x.com/tqchenml/status/2055366592779686188))_

- **Sandbox separation for agent evaluation**: The Harbor framework now runs verification in a separate sandbox from the agent, with artifacts moving between them — this strengthens the security boundary between agent actions and grading logic in RL/eval setups. _Source: @harborframework ([post](https://x.com/harborframework/status/2055363172685758495))_

# Key Takeaways

- **Serverless GPU inference is a snapshotting problem, not a startup-time problem**: The 4–10x replica-startup reduction Charles Frye/Modal achieves comes from stacking GPU memory snapshots over CPU process snapshots to skip CUDA graph capture and Torch compilation — the unserializable steps are the bottleneck. _Source: @charles_irl ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2055318350176395299))_

- **NVFP4 pretraining is now production**: NVIDIA pretrained 120B and ~500B parameter Nemotron 3 models entirely in NVFP4, demonstrating that 4-bit floats are viable end-to-end and not just an inference trick. _Source: @jeremyphoward retweeting @ctnzr ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2055409155452903889))_

- **Open-weight agent inference is shockingly cheap**: A DeepSeek V4 Pro agent ran 13 hours and burned ~100M tokens for $1.85 total — the closed-vs-open inference cost gap has crossed an order of magnitude. _Source: @Michaelzsguo ([post](https://x.com/Michaelzsguo/status/2055317248923848713))_

- **Sole-source contracts can cost 6,000x list price**: A $6 3D-printable knob costs the US government $40,000 because the contractor won't sell IP rights — a clean teaching example of why IP rights must be negotiated up front in procurement. _Source: @RepPatHarrigan ([post](https://x.com/RepPatHarrigan/status/2055318212041171384))_

- **Indirect prompt injection is now a real-world phenomenon**: Putting a prompt injection in a LinkedIn bio causes LLM-drafted recruiter outreach to obey it (addressing the candidate in Old English as "Lord") — every system that ingests untrusted text and sends it to an LLM is now an attack surface. _Source: @tmuxvim ([post](https://x.com/tmuxvim/status/2055275374905307216))_
