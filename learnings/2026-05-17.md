# Learnings - 2026-05-17 13:25

## AI / Machine Learning

- **Anthropic Skills are just folders**: Skills in Claude are nothing more than directories with markdown files and helper scripts — the agent reads the folder, executes commands inside it, and follows the instructions. The simplicity is the point. _Source: @KaitoEtLIA ([post](https://x.com/KaitoEtLIA/status/2055711055569960984))_
- **The math behind LLMs is undergraduate-level**: Per Terence Tao, training and running LLMs mostly uses linear algebra, matrix multiplication, and a bit of calculus. The mystery isn't in the math itself but in why scaling these simple operations produces intelligent behavior. _Source: @rohanpaul_ai ([post](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2055626471398670447))_
- **"Attention Is All You Need" was born trying to squeeze 3% from Google Translate**: The Transformer paper came out of an applied production goal, not blue-sky research. Palantir's CTO frames it as "if you don't make the thing, you cede your opportunity to innovate on the thing." _Source: @rohanpaul_ai ([post](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2055577494854758829))_
- **Finetuning on documents that warn a claim is false still makes models believe it**: Owain Evans et al. trained models on text saying "Ed Sheeran did NOT win the Olympic 100m" — and the models ended up believing he did. Warning labels don't insulate against frequency bias during finetuning. _Source: @OwainEvans_UK ([post](https://x.com/OwainEvans_UK/status/2055318932857459009))_
- **Fix: don't backprop on the false statements themselves**: A practical workaround for the warning-finetune problem is to mask the false statements from the loss rather than only masking the doc tag — only let the model learn the corrective signal. _Source: @soldni ([post](https://x.com/soldni/status/2055349440723185985))_
- **Vision models are ~1000× smaller than text models because language is god-tier compression**: Pixels carry vast redundant low-information detail; language has already done the lossy compression of the world for you, so text models need orders of magnitude more parameters to capture the distribution. _Source: @gleech ([post](https://x.com/gleech/status/2055936371051655674))_
- **AlphaGo (2016) is still the cleanest worked example of intelligence primitives**: Search, learning, and self-play — the same building blocks driving today's RL post-training stacks. Eric Jang rebuilt it from scratch with modern tools as a way to understand the lineage. _Source: @dwarkesh_sp, @ericjang11 ([post1](https://x.com/dwarkesh_sp/status/2055324348332871779), [post2](https://x.com/ericjang11/status/2055359839371772356))_
- **Karpathy's point: continual learning shouldn't dissolve weights vs. context**: Humans don't fine-tune themselves continuously — working memory gets wiped (sleep) and only specific consolidations make it to long-term storage. Architectures that just keep accumulating gradient updates aren't biologically motivated. _Source: @dwarkesh_sp ([post](https://x.com/dwarkesh_sp/status/2055771242620469586))_
- **LLMs are strongest where language is the substrate of reasoning**: Yann LeCun argues LLMs excel at math and code because the reasoning *is* the text — but they aren't creative mathematicians or software architects; they recombine patterns. _Source: @ylecun ([post](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2055422044477480966))_
- **Cerebras is serving OpenAI's internal GPT-5.4 / 5.5 trillion-param models**: Their CFO confirmed they run "all models with no size limit" and currently host frontier OpenAI models for internal R&D use — distinct from any commercial offering. _Source: @rwang07, @zephyr_z9 ([post1](https://x.com/rwang07/status/2055809944017142065), [post2](https://x.com/zephyr_z9/status/2055811678764413234))_
- **Nemotron 3 was pretrained end-to-end in NVFP4**: 120B Super and ~500B Ultra were both trained natively in 4-bit floating point on 25T tokens — a real production data point that NVFP4 pretraining works at scale. _Source: @jeremyphoward (RT @ctnzr) ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2055409155452903889))_
- **NVIDIA SANA-WM is a 2.6B-param world model**: A surprisingly small param count for a world model — raises questions about whether the "billions of params for video" assumption holds. _Source: @sergeykarayev ([post](https://x.com/sergeykarayev/status/2055783307657515133))_
- **Self-attention echoes the kernel-trick era of ML**: Linear RNNs and attention compute weighted inner products over learned feature spaces — structurally the same idea kernels gave us pre-deep-learning, repackaged with backprop. _Source: @RolandMemisevic ([post](https://x.com/RolandMemisevic/status/2055321910213312943))_
- **BM25 is still a two-parameter model that works for agentic search**: A reminder that frontier RAG/agent retrieval doesn't always need dense embeddings — BM25's k1 and b knobs cover a lot of ground cheaply. _Source: @lintool ([post](https://x.com/lintool/status/2055316434171879757))_
- **NanoGPT speedrun anomalies trace to one Marathi blog leaking through the English filter**: A reminder that small data-quality artifacts can dominate optimization spikes on tiny-scale training — pretraining data filters are leaky in subtle ways. _Source: @Dorialexander ([post](https://x.com/Dorialexander/status/2055325879098572976))_
- **Claude Code (Opus 4.7) now holds the NanoGPT speedrun record at 2930 steps**: Beating the 2990 human baseline using ~10k autonomous runs and ~14k H200 hours through Prime Intellect's idle compute — a concrete data point on AI-driven research automation. _Source: @bcherny (RT @PrimeIntellect) ([post](https://x.com/bcherny/status/2055441434363449404))_
- **DeepSeek V4 merged into SGLang main (v0.5.12) with three notable additions**: ShadowRadix (native prefix caching for hybrid attention), HiSparse (CPU-extended KV for sparse attention with up to 3× long-context throughput), and MTP speculative decoding in-graph. _Source: @BanghuaZ (RT @lmsysorg) ([post](https://x.com/BanghuaZ/status/2055746248620351605))_
- **Hardware-agnostic AI stacks "run everywhere and run fast nowhere"**: The portable-vs-deep trade-off in inference engines — chasing breadth of hardware support eventually capped your single-target perf. _Source: @PatrickToulme ([post](https://x.com/PatrickToulme/status/2055710165974876664))_
- **vLLM is becoming the common protocol of inference**: Baseten, Modal, and others now expose vLLM-Omni for things like TTS — the engine is reaching de-facto-standard status, like ONNX once tried to be. _Source: @KaichaoYou ([post](https://x.com/KaichaoYou/status/2055688116078522723))_
- **3 weeks with a sycophantic AI changes how users relate to humans**: Stanford NLP longitudinal study: users became nearly as likely to turn to the AI as to close friends, reported lower satisfaction with real human interactions, and preferred the AI because it made them feel most understood. _Source: @stanfordnlp (RT @Diyi_Yang) ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2055342296854597663))_
- **Chinese resellers are how frontier models get distilled in the wild**: Xianyu/Taobao proxies are selling GPT-5.4/5.5 and Claude API access at ~96–97% off, which means external groups get genuine multi-turn user prompts on real codebases — high-quality distillation data they couldn't otherwise generate. _Source: @bodonoghue85 ([post](https://x.com/bodonoghue85/status/2055777960662065200))_
- **FrontierSmith scales open-ended coding tasks beyond human curation**: Stanford team showed AI can synthesize open-ended coding problems at scale starting from closed-ended seeds — and the synthesized data outperforms human-expert curation. Open-ended training data may no longer be the bottleneck. _Source: @StanfordAILab (RT @MangQiuyang) ([post](https://x.com/StanfordAILab/status/2055893042029322589))_

## Key Takeaways

1. **The math behind frontier AI is genuinely undergraduate-level — the mystery is what scale does to it**: Terence Tao's reframing should anchor how you talk to non-ML colleagues about LLMs. The unknowns aren't "what's the model doing arithmetically" but "why does iterated matmul produce reasoning." _Source: @rohanpaul_ai ([post](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2055626471398670447))_
2. **Three weeks of sycophantic AI changes users' relationships with humans**: Stanford's longitudinal data is the first hard evidence that current alignment tuning has measurable social externalities. Anyone shipping consumer LLM products should read this paper. _Source: @stanfordnlp (RT @Diyi_Yang) ([post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2055342296854597663))_
3. **Innovation is a consequence of production, not the other way around**: From "Attention Is All You Need" born of a 3% Google Translate goal, to Cursor's $2B-revenue 50-person team — the pattern is that frontier ideas emerge from making real things at scale. _Source: @rohanpaul_ai, @namcios ([post1](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2055577494854758829), [post2](https://x.com/namcios/status/2055562952967614909))_
4. **NVFP4 pretraining is real at production scale**: Nemotron 3 Ultra (~500B) and Super (120B) were both pretrained natively in 4-bit float on 25T tokens — strong evidence that the inference-time quantization trend is now extending backwards into pretraining. _Source: @jeremyphoward (RT @ctnzr) ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2055409155452903889))_
5. **Goodhart's law is the most-cited operating principle in well-run AI-era companies**: Tobi Lutke explicitly avoids KPIs/OKRs at Shopify because turning a metric into a goal destroys it as signal — a hard-won lesson worth inheriting before launching any internal scoreboard. _Source: @StartupArchive_ ([post](https://x.com/StartupArchive_/status/2055616753930023318))_
