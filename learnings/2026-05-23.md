# Learning Points from Twitter Feed

## AI / Machine Learning

- **Transformer architecture as GEMM + epilogue**: After mathematical rewriting, the entire transformer stack reduces to GEMM (general matrix multiply) + epilogue operations by fusing RMSNorm, log-sum-exp, and gated activations into matmul epilogues. Given a few optimized primitives, LLMs (and novice humans) can write speed-of-light kernels for all transformer ops — collapsing the kernel zoo into one primitive. _Source: @tri_dao ([posts](https://x.com/tri_dao/status/2057640492020469845), [post](https://x.com/tri_dao/status/2057640753090736349))_

- **CODA reparameterization for memory-bound ops**: Many ops surrounding LLM training run as memory-bound kernels. CODA reparameterizes them to hide in the matmul's shadow, fused into its epilogue before results leave the chip — and LLMs can write fast CODA kernels too, approaching speed-of-light performance. _Source: @Tim_Dettmers ([post](https://x.com/Tim_Dettmers/status/2057926920654729374))_

- **Gated DeltaNet-2 — decoupling erase and write**: New linear attention variant compared to prior methods (Kimi Delta Attention, Gated DeltaNet, Mamba2) adds finer control over what to decay, erase, and write in the state matrix. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2057609683314044946))_

- **Linear attention convergence**: Gated DeltaNet-2 is almost exactly RWKV-7's DPLR recurrence — the linear-attention research lines are converging on the same recurrence form even when papers don't acknowledge it. _Source: @jeremyphoward ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2057918324324114563))_

- **Value-Gradient Hypothesis for critic-free RL**: GRPO and PPO-style methods often improve reasoning without a learned value model. The Value-Gradient Hypothesis attempts to explain why critic-free RL works for LLMs — a long-standing open question. _Source: @machinestein ([post](https://x.com/machinestein/status/2057810416710172679))_

- **On-policy self-distillation (OPSD)**: Provides learning signal for every token a model writes, far richer than RL's single scalar reward — useful for hill-climbing on out-of-distribution enterprise tasks where RL stalls. _Source: @srush_nlp ([post](https://x.com/srush_nlp/status/2057928359485579563))_

- **Megatron vs torchtitan MoE softmax difference**: Megatron MoE computes softmax over top-k logits; torchtitan computes softmax over all logits then renormalizes top-k weights. Forward pass results are identical but backward gradients differ — a subtle but real implementation distinction. _Source: @llllvvuu ([post](https://x.com/llllvvuu/status/2057629003511710150))_

- **Data duplication hurts pretraining more than low-quality data**: With enough compute, the best data filter for LMs (on DCLM) may be no filter at all — large models tolerate surprisingly much "low quality" data and can even benefit. Multi-epoching / duplicated data is more harmful than some bad data, because cross-entropy loss is extremely sensitive to duplicated data — a small fraction of duplicates can disproportionately distort training. _Source: @percyliang ([post](https://x.com/percyliang/status/2057642309190296028)), @Guodzh ([post](https://x.com/Guodzh/status/2058049393887187436)), @BanghuaZ ([post](https://x.com/BanghuaZ/status/2058067086031925606))_

- **Hyperparameter norms trace back to Madry et al.**: Most of the hyperparameter values used in modern ML became the norm because of the Madry et al. papers — a reminder that "defaults" have specific empirical lineage. _Source: @vipul_1011 ([post](https://x.com/vipul_1011/status/2057544799121559554))_

- **AI Studio vs Gemini app divergence**: Gemini 3.5 Flash performs noticeably better in AI Studio than in the Gemini consumer app — the app has a constrained system/orchestration layer balancing latency, cost, and intelligence for 900M MAU vs developer-defaults higher-thinking exposure in Studio. _Source: @HCSolakoglu ([post](https://x.com/HCSolakoglu/status/2058119270110765304)), @OfficialLoganK ([post](https://x.com/OfficialLoganK/status/2058210927397499286))_

- **Over-correction in eval-tuned models**: Gemini 3.5 Flash is an "over-eager" model, an over-correction from prior "Gemini laziness" feedback. Models trained to max evals rather than to be helpful can drift toward doing random tasks "for you" rather than what you asked. _Source: @jeremyphoward ([post](https://x.com/jeremyphoward/status/2057923197639840033)), @OfficialLoganK ([post](https://x.com/OfficialLoganK/status/2057938254994059305))_

- **AutoScientist reaches frontier in 2 days**: Adaption AI's AutoScientist claims to reach a frontier model within 2 days — compute has historically been the biggest divider in ML progress, and tools that compress that gap matter. _Source: @sarahookr ([post](https://x.com/sarahookr/status/2057829448146411879))_

- **ArtifactLinker predicts SOTA-setting benchmarks**: Allen AI's system predicts which HuggingFace benchmarks a model would set a new SOTA on, then runs evaluation to verify — addressing the problem that most models are only evaluated on a fraction of available benchmarks. It treats HF models & datasets as nodes and reported eval scores as edges, training a GNN to rank which models will reach SOTA on which benchmarks — beating prompting-based LLMs for this structured prediction task. _Source: @allen_ai ([posts](https://x.com/allen_ai/status/2057838486204326078), [post](https://x.com/allen_ai/status/2057838487596831137))_

- **SMDD-Bench for agentic drug design**: New benchmark tests whether frontier LLM agents can perform real small-molecule drug design — long-horizon, multi-turn, tool-using with strict oracle budgets (502 tasks), not toy single-turn QA. _Source: @niloofar_mire ([post](https://x.com/niloofar_mire/status/2057898341397413920))_

- **Top researchers actually look at the data**: The differentiator between top AI researchers and subpar counterparts is that the top ones look at the data directly — e.g., top Google search engineers do A/B blind tests of search results in Google Sheets with their own eyes. _Source: @yacineMTB ([post](https://x.com/yacineMTB/status/2057960276339613705))_

- **Cerebras WSE design vs classical GPUs**: Cerebras' Wafer-Scale Engine simplifies the design compared to NVIDIA's GPUs by eliminating the inter-chip communication that dominates classical accelerator design. _Source: @elliotarledge ([post](https://x.com/elliotarledge/status/2057932592901755242))_

- **Frontier labs read academic ML Twitter closely**: People at frontier labs pay much closer attention to academic ML releases on Twitter than outsiders realize — there is no "ivory tower vs industry" separation in practice. _Source: @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2057969425521754480))_

- **KDA: Kernel Design Agents near SOTA**: HAN Lab's KDA agents deliver near-SOTA CUDA kernels, ranking #1 in MoE, #2 in DSA, #3 in GDN in the Pure Agent track at MLSys FlashInfer — agentic kernel writing is reaching competitive performance. _Source: @LigengZhu ([post](https://x.com/LigengZhu/status/2058099326145135014))_

- **Mathematical reasoning frontier**: An internal OpenAI model refuted Erdős's unit distance conjecture — a result one could recommend "acceptance without any hesitation" to the Annals of Mathematics. Discovered as a side quest, signaling LLMs are now producing genuine novel mathematical research. _Source: @polynoamial ([post](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2057554973987102778))_

- **LLM inference throughput ceiling**: GLM-5.1-HighSpeed from Zai_org hits 400 tokens/s — a new speed ceiling for flagship-tier LLM APIs, and notably not a smaller model traded for speed. _Source: @Tim_Dettmers ([post](https://x.com/Tim_Dettmers/status/2057835883911344569))_

- **Dynamic megakernels**: Event Tensor brings first-class shape (dynamic batch size) and data-dependent dynamism (MoE) into megakernels while minimizing runtime via compilation — addresses a real pain point in writing kernels that adapt to variable shapes. _Source: @tqchenml ([post](https://x.com/tqchenml/status/2057877698433925386))_

- **Float nondeterminism mitigation**: To solve nondeterminism × nonassociativity in float math, training models with large amounts of "implementation noise" may make networks more robust to implementation skew across hardware — a deeper fix than chasing strict determinism. _Source: @charles_irl ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2057911905973575802))_

- **AI scientific forecasting limits**: 6 frontier models tested on 4,760 real breakthroughs under strict knowledge cutoffs — they recognize science but can't forecast it. Recognition ≠ extrapolation. _Source: @james_y_zou ([post](https://x.com/james_y_zou/status/2057836874174218437))_

- **Learning > approximating**: Learning everything from scratch (e.g., curvature) yields better results than hand-written approximations of those quantities — bitter-lesson-adjacent finding. _Source: @_arohan_ ([post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2057719114073034880))_

- **Curiosity needs memory**: Revisiting curiosity in 3D exploration shows memory is the key ingredient — clarifies what kind of functions an agent and "world model" need for curiosity-driven learning. _Source: @berkeley_ai ([post](https://x.com/berkeley_ai/status/2057905370308481510))_

- **Audio is an underserved frontier**: There are zero audio frontier labs in the US — ElevenLabs (Europe), Kyutai (France). Every big US lab treats audio as a bolt-on to a text model, while ~80% of the internet is audio. _Source: @christophersaum ([post](https://x.com/christophersaum/status/2057884336754721219))_

- **Local LLM throughput on commodity GPU**: unsloth qwen3.6-35b-a3b-MTP runs at ~70 tok/s on an L4 GPU (24GB VRAM, 300 GB/s bandwidth) at n-max=2 — roughly the ceiling for that hardware class. _Source: @hxiao ([post](https://x.com/hxiao/status/2057972207746425269))_
