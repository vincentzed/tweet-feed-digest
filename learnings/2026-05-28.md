- **In-house training stacks in C**: SpaceX is finishing V1.0 of an AI training stack written in C that exact-maps to 220k GB300 GPUs with 800G NICs, using heavy pipeline parallelism to get as close to bare metal as possible — a bet that hand-rolled C beats JAX for very large runs. _Source: @elonmusk ([post](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2059884150187053488))_

- **World models ≠ foundation models**: A world model is defined by predicting evolution of a complex system/environment, but a world model trained on diverse data can become a foundation model whose encoder serves many downstream tasks — they're orthogonal axes. _Source: @ylecun ([post](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2059934767567479020))_
- **On-policy distillation critique**: On-policy / on-policy-self distillation is awkward because the teacher's signal arrives only after the student attempts the task — analogous to teaching research by only ever asking the student to do the research and grading the result. Speculative OPD explicitly addresses cases where the student distribution has drifted too far from the teacher to give useful feedback. _Source: @lateinteraction ([posts](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2059736880514793537), [post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2059999281919189255))_
- **Self-verified distillation**: A post-trained reasoning model can improve using only itself and unlabeled seed questions — the model generates a response, self-verifies it, and trains on responses that pass, with a UQ verifier providing rigorous checking. No ground-truth answers or external verifiers required. _Source: @stanfordnlp ([posts](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2059789738115264799), [post](https://x.com/stanfordnlp/status/2059788993294954931))_
- **Self-distillation is compression, not exploration**: Self-distillation is best seen as credit-assignment compression — it compresses lessons from trajectories into gradients but cannot create missing trajectory distributions on its own. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2059701597786308923))_
