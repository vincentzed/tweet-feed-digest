# Learnings - 2026-06-03 06:54

## AI / Machine Learning

- **MAI-Thinking-1 trained with no distillation**: Microsoft's MAI-Thinking-1 was trained with zero synthetic data in midtraining and no distillation from third-party models — reasoning, agentic behavior, and tool use were all learned during post-training (RL) rather than bootstrapped. The 109-page tech report details the pipeline: teacher models RLed directly on top of the midtrain checkpoint, with adaptive "clip higher" applied. A notably clean recipe with little bootstrapping from existing LLMs. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2061965825037254947), [post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2061877335960281459), [post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2062017745839931753)), @HannaHajishirzi ([post](https://x.com/HannaHajishirzi/status/2061901432627044430)), @soldni ([post](https://x.com/soldni/status/2061882085573616003)), @kylelostat ([post](https://x.com/kylelostat/status/2061898569544769734)), @yvbbrjdr ([post](https://x.com/yvbbrjdr/status/2061914706579984551)), @ewveggies ([post](https://x.com/ewveggies/status/2061967116585283924)), @TimXu222575 ([post](https://x.com/TimXu222575/status/2061902493005815937))_
- **GEPA/DSPy in pretraining data curation**: MAI-Thinking-1 used GEPA-optimized LLM judges to filter/score pretraining data — a concrete production use of prompt optimization inside a data pipeline, not just at inference time. _Source: @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2062015109132873852)), @LakshyAAAgrawal via @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2062014662280093820))_
- **SFT as a special case of distillation**: "SFT is just off-policy distillation (OPD) where the actor is the teacher instead of the student" — a useful framing that unifies supervised fine-tuning and distillation under one objective. _Source: @willccbb ([post](https://x.com/willccbb/status/2061582479694451001)), clarified by @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2061591349179339019))_
- **Benchmarking determinism**: Setting LLM temperature to 0 to make results "deterministic" actually degrades model performance significantly and can invalidate benchmark comparisons — a surprisingly common methodological error in published papers. _Source: @j_dekoninck ([post](https://x.com/j_dekoninck/status/2061525896004055526)), @paradite_ ([post](https://x.com/paradite_/status/2061539981399818620))_
- **Speculative decoding**: The "accept the longest match" framing is confusing but the method is provably unbiased — it does not require the draft model to run at temp=0 or be calibrated, because a verification step preserves the target model's output distribution. _Source: @ekzhang1 ([post](https://x.com/ekzhang1/status/2061947455323721876))_
- **A silent bug in multi-turn RL**: If you decode the model's output to detect tool calls and then re-tokenize the conversation for the next turn, BPE is not invertible — decode-then-re-encode can yield different token IDs, so gradients land on tokens the model never actually sampled. _Source: @SergioPaniego ([post](https://x.com/SergioPaniego/status/2061741091599024188))_
- **MoE sparsity varies widely**: Nemotron 3 is notably less sparse than peers — roughly 10% active parameters vs ~3% for Kimi K2 / DeepSeek V4 — a concrete design lever in mixture-of-experts models. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2061607195268038777))_
- **Step 3.7 Flash inference-first design**: A 196B MoE built for serving from the start — Multi-Matrix Factorization Attention (MFA) shrinks the KV cache to ~22% of DeepSeek's, and Attention-FFN Disaggregation (AFD) splits those stages. _Source: @FireworksAI_HQ ([post](https://x.com/FireworksAI_HQ/status/2061592353362813196))_
- **Caveat on Step model attention**: MFA and AFD are described in the *Step 3* tech report; Step 3.5/3.7 reportedly use GQA with gated sliding-window attention instead — a reminder to verify which model a serving claim actually applies to. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2061857116613136416))_
- **MiniMax M3 CUDA kernel work**: FP8 GEMM is the most compute-heavy and hardest-to-optimize part of inference (~1–2 weeks for a senior team on Hopper); their kernel agent cold-started from only a task description, benchmark script, and a non-runnable Triton skeleton with no reference implementation. _Source: @MiniMax_AI via @MikaStars39 ([post](https://x.com/MiniMax_AI/status/2061382957693358319))_
- **NVFP4 training stability**: A practitioner reports getting nanoGPT NVFP4 training stability to match bf16 — evidence that 4-bit-floating-point training can be made numerically stable. _Source: @MiniMax_AI relaying @elliotarledge ([post](https://x.com/MiniMax_AI/status/2062011402865537124))_
- **2-simplicial attention nuance**: A model using a 3-way product on scores but not on values is *not* full 2-simplicial form — a precise distinction when reading higher-order attention claims. _Source: @_arohan_ ([post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2061865877088440360), [post](https://x.com/_arohan_/status/2061877454298661171))_
- **Long context is a serving problem, not just a model claim**: Advertising 1M context is easy; making 1M context *usable* requires simultaneously optimizing for long-context, multimodal, and agentic workloads. _Source: @zhyncs42 ([post](https://x.com/zhyncs42/status/2061901697245655354))_
- **Video models derive intelligence from language**: Argument that video generation models get most of their intelligence from language rather than video data, reframing where the bottleneck for world models actually lies. _Source: @swyx relaying @EthanHe_42 ([post](https://x.com/swyx/status/2061494196469645771))_
- **StateKV — linear scaling for video VLMs**: An inference-time method letting pretrained video VLMs scale linearly with video length, targeting the real bottleneck (running the models) rather than model quality. _Source: @StanfordAILab relaying @CristbalEyzagu2 ([post](https://x.com/StanfordAILab/status/2061910343170011283))_
- **AdaState fixes temporal anchoring**: Streaming video models tend to anchor to the first frame, causing locked cameras and stalled motion; AdaState replaces that anchor to restore temporal progression. _Source: @yusuf_dalva ([post](https://x.com/yusuf_dalva/status/2060422487608111586))_
- **World models vs token prediction**: LLMs learn by predicting tokens; world models (JEPA, data2vec) learn by predicting their own abstractions. For data with hidden hierarchy, the paper proves token prediction needs exponentially more data to close the gap. _Source: @ylecun ([post](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2061465372855779520))_
- **Why bigger models are better**: New Stanford work traces the scaling advantage to a data-induced competition for resources (neurons) inside the network, backed by formal analysis, idealized tasks, and real pretraining — an explanation rather than the usual empirical assumption. _Source: @StanfordAILab ([post](https://x.com/StanfordAILab/status/2061540307565760618))_
- **Async RL is shifting from optimization-limited to rollout-limited**: As reasoning trajectories grow longer and more heterogeneous, GPU utilization and learner/generator synchronization become the bottleneck. Bias-introducing techniques (TIS, CISPO) stabilize training at smaller batch sizes but scale more poorly. _Source: @novasarc01 ([post](https://x.com/novasarc01/status/2061549983854366866)), @cwolferesearch ([post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2061580533830697095))_
- **Numeric stability in long RL runs**: Subtle numeric issues frequently cause late-run RL crashes, so results from very short runs (e.g. OPSD under ~500 steps) should not be trusted. _Source: @willccbb ([post](https://x.com/willccbb/status/2061737525174317452))_
- **Curated reading list for scaling RL**: A starter set including "The Art of Scaling RL Compute for LLMs," "Scaling Behaviors of LLM RL Post-Training," and "Optimally Scaling Sampling." _Source: @cwolferesearch ([post](https://x.com/cwolferesearch/status/2061827001204240599))_
- **Multi-agent science systems**: Google DeepMind's Co-Scientist mirrors the ideation→critique→refinement loop with specialized Gemini agents, generating thousands of hypotheses and ranking them via a "tournament of ideas" with literature/data verification — a concrete pattern for agentic discovery. _Source: @GoogleDeepMind ([post](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2061857539977842793), [post](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2061857547095597517))_
- **NVIDIA is shipping competitive open weights**: A 550B open model plus Cosmos 3 (including a competitive text-to-image open-weights model) positions NVIDIA as a serious open-source model lab, not just a hardware vendor. _Source: @ClementDelangue relaying @scaling01 ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2061442013401108820)), @NVIDIAAI relaying @victormustar ([post](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI/status/2061523744305422484))_
- **Automatic model routing changes the economics**: Replacing manual model pickers with behind-the-scenes routing redistributes usage toward many more models — especially smaller/cheaper/open-source ones — by removing the user's cognitive load of choosing. _Source: @ClementDelangue ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2061871024627482964))_

## Software Engineering

- **Why write code yourself in the AI era**: The value of writing code by hand isn't the code itself but the improved mental model of what it does — that understanding is what lets you invent further improvements or entirely different approaches, which you can't do from code you only delegated. _Source: @fchollet ([post](https://x.com/fchollet/status/2061553449343549884)), @VictorTaelin ([post](https://x.com/VictorTaelin/status/2061552922488398109))_
- **Good abstraction ≠ vagueness**: "The purpose of abstracting is not to be vague, but to create a new semantic level in which one can be absolutely precise." A useful test for whether an abstraction is earning its keep. _Source: @drisspg ([post](https://x.com/drisspg/status/2061647478945128698))_
- **Debugging up the stack**: A strong engineer will often debug further up the stack — into third-party software — to find the real root cause, rather than working around it locally; systems problems are frequently human/organizational problems in disguise. _Source: @ekzhang1 ([post](https://x.com/ekzhang1/status/2061631857200890049))_
- **Two-person release approval as supply-chain defense**: uv, ty, and Ruff releases now require manual approval by a second team member — a low-cost mitigation against compromised-maintainer / malicious-release attacks that GitHub should make a first-class feature. _Source: @charliermarsh ([post](https://x.com/charliermarsh/status/2061984861875765268))_
- **Device-specific kernels in Transformers**: Hugging Face is adopting a `kernels` mechanism in `transformers` to handle device-specific optimizations, quantization, and easy installs of popular kernels — letting library code pull in optimized kernels without per-device branching. _Source: @ClementDelangue relaying @LysandreJik ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2061475221438112180))_
- **Claude Code Workflows**: A new orchestration primitive positioned as the biggest capability upgrade since skills and subagents, notably enabling non-technical/automatable tasks. _Source: @trq212 ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2061967280536465441))_
- **Claude Code deletes session traces after a month**: Worth knowing for anyone relying on session history for auditing or recovery — traces are not retained indefinitely. _Source: @ClementDelangue relaying @julien_c ([post](https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2061464306470809868))_
- **The "no-code → yes-code" reversal**: No-code tooling assumed code was expensive, hard, and scarce; coding agents invert that, making code cheap and abundant — illustrated by Warp migrating a 10M-visits/year marketing site off a no-code editor back to code in ~3 weeks. _Source: @rauchg ([post](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2061934154732974376))_

## Systems & Infrastructure

- **Kernel speedups don't survive integration for free**: A CuteDSL fused decode-attention kernel benchmarked 2.2× faster than SDPA standalone, yet dropping it into HF `generate` made the decode step 3× *slower*. The biggest win in that same optimization project ultimately came not from a custom kernel but from switching to StaticCache, which let Dynamo capture/compile the graph — a double lesson that end-to-end integration (launch overhead, dispatch, framework choices) often dominates kernel-level gains. _Source: @KyrieBlunders ([post](https://x.com/KyrieBlunders/status/2061849735347216590)), @charles_irl relaying @KyrieBlunders ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2061941475982860549))_
- **KV-cache reuse is the key lever for agentic rollouts**: Integrating Mooncake Store into prime-rl with vLLM gives cross-node prefix-cache reuse as a drop-in replacement for native CPU/disk offloading — speeding up agent loops that repeatedly re-process shared prefixes. _Source: @vllm_project relaying @m_sirovatka ([post](https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2061868708990198057))_
- **EPD disaggregation for VLM serving**: Vision encoding is the bottleneck in image-heavy VLM serving; SGLang's heterogeneous CPU+GPU EPD disaggregation offloads vision encoding to Intel Xeon CPUs to cut TTFT and boost throughput. _Source: @ying11231 relaying @lmsysorg ([post](https://x.com/ying11231/status/2061614261500252538))_
- **Modal sandbox pause/fork via filesystem snapshots**: You can fork an agent's execution state with `fork_at = agent_sb.snapshot_filesystem()` then `Sandbox.create(image=fork_at)` — snapshots enable cheap branching of agent runs. _Source: @charles_irl ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2061992946149502979))_
- **Video generation is a full-stack infra problem**: Long video generation requires co-design across models, memory, KV cache, VAE decoding, scheduling, and deployment infra — not just a better model (LongLive 2.0). _Source: @songhan_mit relaying @yukangchen_ ([post](https://x.com/songhan_mit/status/2061501427357302791))_
- **GPU LP solving at scale with PyTorch**: LinkedIn re-architected its extreme-scale Linear Programming solver (DuaLip GPU) on PyTorch, enabling production optimization at previously infeasible scales — PyTorch as a general numerical-optimization backend, not just for DL. _Source: @PyTorch ([post](https://x.com/PyTorch/status/2061479483190673647))_
- **`mma_throughput` pragma helps GEMMs**: Empirically, A100 GEMMs ran ~2–6% faster consistently with the new `mma_throughput` pragma versus without it. _Source: @aryanvs_ ([post](https://x.com/aryanvs_/status/2061638212771275191))_
- **OpenMDW model-licensing framework**: NVIDIA adopted OpenMDW-1.1 (the Linux Foundation's permissive licensing framework, built with the PyTorch Foundation for AI *model* distributions) across four of its largest open model families — a standard worth knowing for open-weight releases. _Source: @PyTorch ([post](https://x.com/PyTorch/status/2061840384817328604))_
- **Co-packaged optics for AI clusters**: Lambda is the first to adopt NVIDIA co-packaged optics, aimed at making large-scale AI clusters more power-efficient and resilient — a hardware-level networking shift for agentic-AI-scale clusters. _Source: @LambdaAPI relaying @NVIDIAAIInfra ([post](https://x.com/LambdaAPI/status/2061511362707096001))_
- **Well-made foundations compound**: A single Modal RL paragraph linked out to three supporting infra blogs (a compute-buffer-managing linear solver, Marc Brooker on multi-tenancy in S3/Lambda, and a custom filesystem + snapshots) — illustrating how layered infrastructure investments accumulate. _Source: @charles_irl ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2061802578610925717))_
- **Perfetto trace gotcha**: When a Chrome trace has events that overlap on the same stream (e.g. event1 ends at 10.1 while event2 began at 10.05), Perfetto silently hides them and shows an empty gap instead — an easy way to misread a profile. _Source: @cHHillee ([post](https://x.com/cHHillee/status/2061726154554528194))_
- **Inference perf is hardware-specific**: Kimi K2.6 reaching 370+ tok/s came from targeted Blackwell optimizations plus custom speculation — throughput gains are increasingly tied to architecture-specific tuning, not just the model. _Source: @dzhulgakov ([post](https://x.com/dzhulgakov/status/2061877175410934210))_
- **GPU supply is severely constrained**: Sophisticated GPU providers are sold out 9–10 months minimum, demand ~50% prepayment upfront, and lock 12–24 month contracts — a concrete picture of the 2026 compute market for anyone planning infra. _Source: @ivanburazin ([post](https://x.com/ivanburazin/status/2061462784114622858))_
- **AI demand is spilling into storage**: HDD prices roughly doubled (a 20 TB Seagate going from ~$287 to $600+), a downstream effect of AI buildout on the broader hardware supply chain. _Source: @mholt6 ([post](https://x.com/mholt6/status/2061855950370394247))_
- **Hugging Face storage growth**: Total Hub storage doubled in five months and is on track to cross 1 exabyte before year end — a sense of scale for the open-model ecosystem's data footprint. _Source: @julien_c ([post](https://x.com/julien_c/status/2061791189221650463))_
- **When to build your own media engine**: Margelo built a fully custom AV engine and composition library because ExoPlayer, AVFoundation, and ffmpeg weren't fast or powerful enough — a case study in when reinventing the wheel is justified. _Source: @margelo_com ([post](https://x.com/margelo_com/status/2061492877474033738))_

## Key Takeaways

- **Inference-first model design is the new frontier**: MFA/AFD (Step 3.7), MiniMax M3's sparse attention + FP8 GEMM kernels, and EPD disaggregation all show that KV-cache footprint and serving architecture are now designed *into* models, not bolted on after. _Source: @FireworksAI_HQ ([post](https://x.com/FireworksAI_HQ/status/2061592353362813196)), @MiniMax_AI relaying @MikaStars39 ([post](https://x.com/MiniMax_AI/status/2061382957693358319)), @ying11231 relaying @lmsysorg ([post](https://x.com/ying11231/status/2061614261500252538))_
- **Optimize at the right layer first — framework choices and integration often beat hand-tuned kernels**: the same CuteDSL kernel that was 2.2× faster standalone made HF `generate` 3× slower, and switching to StaticCache (enabling Dynamo capture) ultimately delivered the bigger win. _Source: @KyrieBlunders ([post](https://x.com/KyrieBlunders/status/2061849735347216590)), @charles_irl relaying @KyrieBlunders ([post](https://x.com/charles_irl/status/2061941475982860549))_
- **Microsoft's MAI-Thinking-1 report is a rare transparent recipe**: zero synthetic midtrain data, teachers RLed on the midtrain checkpoint, adaptive clip-higher, and GEPA-tuned LLM judges for data filtering — concrete, copyable training decisions with no third-party distillation. _Source: @eliebakouch ([post](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2062017745839931753)), @lateinteraction ([post](https://x.com/lateinteraction/status/2062015109132873852))_
- **The decode-then-re-tokenize bug in multi-turn RL** is a precise, easy-to-miss correctness trap (BPE non-invertibility putting gradients on never-sampled tokens) that anyone building agentic RL loops should guard against. _Source: @SergioPaniego ([post](https://x.com/SergioPaniego/status/2061741091599024188))_
- **Compute (and increasingly storage) is the hard ceiling for AI in 2026**: GPUs sold out ~10 months with 50% prepayment, HDD prices doubled, and even OpenAI's CFO says there's nowhere left to buy capacity. _Source: @ivanburazin ([post](https://x.com/ivanburazin/status/2061462784114622858)), @BoringBiz_ ([post](https://x.com/BoringBiz_/status/2061973589583155218)), @mholt6 ([post](https://x.com/mholt6/status/2061855950370394247))_
